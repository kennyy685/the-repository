# Improvement research

Goal: ideas that get HMP more leads and more signed jobs, backed by evidence, sized so FilthE can
pick. HMP is small, bilingual (Spanish/English), new to insurance restoration, and on a tight budget.

## 1. Know the baseline (5 minutes)
Read `CLAUDE.md`: the storm engine, Hot Zones, door lists, the command center, Crew HQ, HMP HQ,
the board. Don't recommend what already exists; say how to make it better instead.

## 2. Where to look (best first)
- Primary sources: official docs and product pages (NOAA/NWS, Census, Nebraska statutes at
  nebraskalegislature.gov, tool vendors' own feature pages).
- Practitioners: roofing/restoration trade publications, contractor associations, experienced
  canvassers' guides and training, software comparison sites (note they are often sponsored).
- AI/automation: Anthropic's docs and engineering posts for anything about Claude or agents.
Skip forums with no evidence, AI-written listicles, and anything selling a "guaranteed" result.
Open (WebFetch) the pages you rely on; a search snippet alone is not a source.

## 3. Judge every idea
- Evidence: what shows it works (numbers, named companies, official guidance)? Say "unproven" if so.
- Fit: does it work for a small bilingual crew in eastern Nebraska, on a small budget?
- Legal: Nebraska storm-contractor law (Neb. Rev. Stat. 44-8601 to 44-8611): never waive/cover/rebate
  a deductible, never negotiate claims (public adjuster license), 3-day cancel right, deductible
  notice in contracts. Door-to-door permits per town (HMP has them). Do-Not-Call and TCPA rules for
  calls and texts: no cold texting, no bought lists. Drop any idea that bends these.
- Cost: free / cheap / paid (give the price if the page shows it).

## 4. Report format (return exactly this, under 500 words)
```
TOPIC: <the topic>
TOP IDEAS (best payoff for effort first, 3-6 of them):
1. <idea in one line>
   Why it helps HMP: <one or two lines, concrete>
   Evidence: <what shows it works> [source numbers]
   Effort: S / M / L   Cost: free / $ / $$   Who: FilthE / Code / Cowork / the boss
QUICK TIPS (things FilthE can do this week, no build needed): <2-4 bullets>
WATCH OUT: <risks, legal lines, hype to ignore>
SOURCES: [1] <title> - <url>  (only pages you opened)
```
Plain English, short sentences, no jargon. FilthE reads this on his phone.

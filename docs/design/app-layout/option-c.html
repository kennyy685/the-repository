Field / Office

Two modes, one switch at the top. Field is one screen for the street: the door, 4 huge buttons, the Sale Guide words already showing, and a talk bar. Office is for mornings and nights: Plan · Leads · Money.

  • Field has no tabs at all: nothing to hunt for with gloves on, in low sun.
  • Translation: "Say it in Spanish" is one of the 3 talk-bar buttons, always one tap.
  • Plan holds tomorrow's follow-ups, tomorrow's walk and the end-of-day review.
  • More (boss view, Practice Door, print, prices) → the K button.

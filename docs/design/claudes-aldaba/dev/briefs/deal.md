# Brief: deal

Files: js/deal.js, css/deal.css (replace the stub)

YOU OWN #deal: one door's path from first knock to a signed job, with Nebraska's legal armor. The home is A.dealHome if set (from Knock; listen for 'deal:home'), else NL.homes rank 1 (3944 21 St, a FAKE sample).
CONTENT (use COPY.deal: labels, steps[8] {id,title,collect[],law{cite,plain}|null,next,done_when, also[], rule}, armor[12], homeowner {...}): a horizontal (at 400 px: vertical) TRACK of the 8 steps (intro at the door; look + photos; inspection; meet the adjuster; itemized description; contract + 3-day cancel notice; cancel window; build) showing where this door is (default: step 3 inspection set), each step clickable. The selected step panel shows: what to collect (a checklist the salesman ticks, persisted per viewer in A.store), what's legally required with the cite tag (A.ui.srcTag('law') style + the statute number), what's next, and done-when. The legal armor strip lists the 12 armor rules compactly with cites. Tone: legal armor, not coaching.
CENTER PIECE: a large House.animate portrait of the selected home (the generative architectural drawing: lines, then shingles, then hail landing as knock rings, then mono callouts localized EN/ES via labels), with the highlight following the step (roof on inspection, siding etc.). Place it floating over the dimmed map (the map camera sits close on the home) or in the right/center slot, whichever composes best at 1440x900; make it the hero.
HOMEOWNER SHEET: a "Hand to homeowner" button opens a modal (in #slot-overlay) previewing the homeowner-facing sheet branded HMP Siding & Roofing (never Aldaba), EN and ES side by side (COPY.deal.homeowner): what happens next, the 3-day right to cancel in plain words, mailing address 2600 Laverna St, Apt 50, Fremont, NE 68025, "Nebraska contractor registration #: ____" blank line, a signature line. Printed-paper feel (Ledger light surface even in dark theme, via tokens), Escape closes, focus trapped and restored. No phone numbers.
Also a small CANCEL WINDOW clock for the contract step: signed date -> 3 business days (skip Sunday and holidays per COPY) with the "no work before it ends on a non-insurance sale (69-1606(5))" line; for insurance work the 44-8606 itemized description to homeowner AND insurer is a required check before build.
MOTION THAT MEANS SOMETHING: the track fills to the current step like a fuse, the step panel lands with the hail spring, ticking a checklist item rings out, the house draws itself on entry. Expose A.dealDemo = {step(i), open()} so the director can drive it.

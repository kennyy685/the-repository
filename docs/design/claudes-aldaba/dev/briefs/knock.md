# Brief: knock

Files: js/knock.js, css/knock.css (replace the stub)

YOU OWN #knock: the street-level walk of the pick (Columbus, 22 St & 21 St; NL.walk: park point, streets s[] with names/homes/lines; NL.homes: 25 FAKE sample homes with rank, addr, st, p, built, roof years, own, hail, score).
THE MAP AT STREET ZOOM IS TOO EMPTY TODAY (shots/knock-dark-en-1440.png: bare grey streets and number circles). Make it dense, real and beautiful: generate deterministic sample lot lines and house footprints along the Columbus streets in view (both sides of each street, ~55-75 ft lots, footprints sized/rotated to the street, driveways; seeded by street name so they never change; clearly part of the "sample homes" layer), the 25 walk homes as brighter footprints with a door ring (the knocker ring) colored by door score / hail, the modeled hail field tinting the block (A.world.hail.at), the park spot "P", and the walk route drawn in walk order (animated dash that becomes solid as doors get done). Canvas2D layers are fine; keep 60 fps.
LEFT PANEL: the walk street by street (22 St, 21 St, 40 Ave with door counts), live counters (knocked / talked / inspections set / come back) that roll, and the CURRENT DOOR card: address, built, roof age, hail at this door (radar estimate, not confirmed at this address), owner-lived (sample), door score, a small House.draw portrait of that sample home, sample tag. A legal check at the door: "At the door first: your name, HMP, what we sell (Neb. 69-1602)" as a checkbox, never a script (COPY.knock.legal). ONE TAP PER DOOR: a big outcome bar No answer / Talked / Inspection set / Not interested / Come back (keys 1-5 are taken by tabs, so use N T I X B, shown as kbd hints; COPY.knock.outcomes); tapping ripples the door on the map (the knock), plays Sound.knock() if Sound.enabled, marks it, advances to the next door, and the camera eases to it; "Inspection set" opens a small inline follow-up (pick a time from 3 slots) and offers "Open in Deal" (A.view.go('deal') with that home: set A.dealHome = home and emit 'deal:home'). Outcomes persist per viewer in A.store with an Undo and a Reset walk. Evening recap strip when all doors are done (COPY.knock.recap). Clicking any door on the map selects it.
MOTION THAT MEANS SOMETHING: entering the view, the route draws from the park spot in walk order and the doors light up one by one along it (stagger in walk order), counters roll; each tap = ripple + the next door lifts. Expose A.knockDemo = {tap(outcome), select(rank)} so the director can drive it.

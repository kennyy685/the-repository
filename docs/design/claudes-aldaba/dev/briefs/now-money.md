MONEY = the path to FilthE's goal of $100,000 by Dec 31, 2026 (today is Sep 29). Use Funnel.plan(). Be honest: at typical new-rep industry rates the goal needs about 285 doors a day, far over his ~42 doors in 3.5 h. Don't hide that and don't make it depressing: make it the most useful screen in the app. Show (a) a reverse funnel $100k -> jobs -> signed -> inspections -> booked -> conversations -> doors -> doors per day, each step with its rate and a source tag (industry benchmarks, vendor figures; replace with HMP's own after ~200 doors), numbers rolling on change; (b) the LEVERS as controls FilthE can drag (commission per job = "example, set by HMP" slider; close-rate stage new rep / typical / experienced; hours per day; knock days per week) with the doors-per-day answer updating live and a clear "fits in your day" vs "over your day" state; (c) a progress ring toward $100k (the knocker ring motif) and "What it takes this week"; (d) a map layer: rings over the ranked zones sized by how many of the needed doors each zone can supply (NL.zones homes), map dimmed back. Never promise earnings, never say insurance pays; the average payout is an industry figure. Levers remember per viewer (A.store).

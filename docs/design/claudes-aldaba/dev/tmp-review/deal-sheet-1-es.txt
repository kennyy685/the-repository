What happens next

Thank you for having us look at your home.

Home: 3944 21 St

1

Today

We looked at your home and took photos. You can see any of them on our phone.

2

Your insurance company

If you want to report the damage, you contact your insurance company. Only your insurance company decides what your policy covers.

3

The adjuster's visit

If you ask, we come along to show what we found. We do not negotiate or settle your claim.

4

A written description

Before any repair starts, you get an itemized description of the work: materials, labor, fees and the total. On a job tied to an insurance claim, your insurance company gets the same one.

5

Your contract

If you choose to work with HMP, you sign a written contract. You get a copy and two cancel forms.

6

Your 3 business days

You can change your mind during this time. If your job is not part of an insurance claim, we start no work until this time ends.

7

The work

We schedule the work and get the permits. We clean up each work day and walk the finished job with you.

YOUR RIGHT TO CANCEL

You can cancel this agreement until midnight of the third business day after you sign it.

To cancel, mail a written note to HMP Siding & Roofing LLC, 2600 Laverna St, Apt 50, Fremont, NE 68025. Any written note that says you do not want the agreement will work. The cancel form in your contract is the easiest way. A note counts the day you mail it.

If your job is paid from an insurance claim, you can also cancel until midnight of the third business day after your insurance company tells you in writing that any part is not covered, if that date is later.

If you cancel on time, HMP returns your payments within 10 days.

Su contrato

Sus 3 días hábiles

Usted puede cambiar de opinión durante este tiempo. Si su trabajo no es parte de un reclamo de seguro, no empezamos ningún trabajo hasta que termine este plazo.

7

El trabajo

Programamos el trabajo y sacamos los permisos. Limpiamos cada día de trabajo y recorremos con usted el trabajo terminado.

SU DERECHO A CANCELAR

Usted puede cancelar este acuerdo hasta la medianoche del tercer día hábil después de firmarlo.

Para cancelar, mande por correo una nota escrita a HMP Siding & Roofing LLC, 2600 Laverna St, Apt 50, Fremont, NE 68025. Sirve cualquier nota escrita que diga que ya no quiere el acuerdo. Si cancela a tiempo, HMP le devuelve sus pagos en un plazo de 10 días.

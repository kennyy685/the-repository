# AI Hub: professional design + research brief (FilthE, 2026-09-28)

FilthE's own brief, saved verbatim in substance. Foundation = the current isometric two-floor office (navy night,
walnut, floor-to-ceiling glass, small robotic agents, stations, labels, a central connection between floors).
Build on it; do not redesign it.

## Core goal
Professional tech company + AI research lab + command center + creative studio + interactive world + game-like
feedback. Feels PROFESSIONAL + FUN + EFFICIENT + INTELLIGENT. Not childish, cartoonish, generic corporate, sterile,
cyberpunk, neon-heavy, a game lobby, or a chatbot dashboard. "THE ENVIRONMENT IS THE INTERFACE": researching shows
research, coding shows coding, testing shows testing, needing the user is obvious. First reaction should be "this is
where an entire team of AI agents works", not "a dashboard" and not "just a video game".
References: Apple cleanliness, IDEO creativity, Gensler workplace planning (incl. NEXT NEXT / future lab research),
Microsoft AI/agent interface design, modern AI + robotics labs, command/control centers, isometric virtual offices,
game UI that shows status through the environment. Principle: organize by TYPES OF WORK (focus, research,
collaboration, experimentation, testing, monitoring, creation, operations, recharge), not rows of desks.

## Floors
- LOWER = EXECUTION (BUILD, CREATE, EXECUTE, FIX, ORGANIZE): Builder, Designer, Claude Code, Right Hand, Engine
  Mechanic, Chat Reader, Cowork (+ utility agents). Workstations, coding/design stations, engineering/testing table,
  collaboration area, central workflow table, prototype area, knowledge wall, small meeting area.
- UPPER = INTELLIGENCE (RESEARCH, ANALYZE, MONITOR, VALIDATE, DISCOVER): Research Lead, QA Tester, Storm Watch.
  Research wall, live data displays, monitoring stations, testing table, observation area, large central display,
  glass walls, quieter.
- CENTRAL CONNECTION (elevator / stair / glass bridge / vertical connector / data pathway / observation platform):
  metaphor INTELLIGENCE -> EXECUTION.

## Agents
Small sophisticated humanoid/robotic characters: premium assistants, not mascots, not photoreal. Each has a
workstation, recognizable role, subtle personality, visual status, occasional movement, relationships. Examples:
Builder "BUILDING... 91%" then walks to QA; Research Lead "SCANNING, 142 SOURCES, 3 RELEVANT FINDINGS"; QA
"TESTING... / TEST FAILED / INVESTIGATING... / FIX VERIFIED".

## Status system
Glanceable: who works, waits, needs me, what's happening, what failed, what finished. Starting idea: green working,
yellow waiting, blue researching, purple thinking, orange needs user, red error; change it if research says better.

## Workflow made visible
Builder finishes -> a visual connection travels BUILDER -> QA -> RIGHT HAND -> "TEST COMPLETE 17/18 PASSED 1 ISSUE".

## Central command / THE OBSERVATORY (favorite)
A circular/central table showing the whole org: ACTIVE AGENTS, RESEARCH/BUILD/QA task counts, NEEDS YOU, SYSTEM
HEALTHY, RECENT EVENT. What the Hub knows, is doing, changed, needs; errors; important events. A signature element.

## Zones
Research lab (information wall, knowledge graphs, source analysis, quiet desks, observation, experiment table: "we
investigate things here"). QA area (testing table, devices, test queue, pass/fail, sandbox, diagnostics). Design
studio (IDEO-like boards, sketches, prototype table, moodboards: creative but organized). Human/recharge corner
(coffee machine, couch, plant, warm light, window, small table; no holograms, no robots everywhere).

## Fun (not childish)
Movement, visible activity, status changes, agents interacting, workflow connections, small environmental
animations, progress, completions, occasional surprises, personality, storytelling (Builder finishes, gets up, walks
to QA, hands off, QA tests, status changes, task moves to Right Hand).

## Materials / lighting / architecture
Dark walnut, warm wood (important), matte black, warm concrete, glass, brushed metal, subtle tech; not all metal.
Deep navy ambient, warm desk lamps, soft white task light, subtle blue accents, small status lights; no excess neon.
Open plans, glass walls, elevation changes, balconies, central stair/elevator, dedicated stations, observation
platforms, quiet rooms, collaboration spaces; keep the isometric view.

## Don't
Generic cyberpunk, neon purple/blue everywhere, spaceship, boring corporate, sterile lab, server room, kids' game,
SaaS dashboard, rows of identical desks, random holograms, tech with no purpose. Every element needs a reason.

## Efficiency first
At any moment answer: what am I working on, what are agents doing, who works, who waits, who needs me, what
recently happened, what needs action, what's researched, built, failed, completed. Reduce cognitive load.

## Deliverable asked for
Research first, then reasoning, then the design system, then concrete visual references, then a detailed blueprint:
1 real-world design refs, 2 AI/robotics lab refs, 3 command-center refs, 4 creative studio refs, 5 workplace
research, 6 AI-agent interface research, 7 virtual/isometric offices, 8 game environments that show workflow,
9 specific visual refs, 10 architecture, 11 floor plan, 12 materials, 13 lighting, 14 character design,
15 status/interaction system, 16 animations, 17 UI elements, 18 fun-not-childish, 19 efficient-not-boring,
20 a complete design language.

## Framing (FilthE, 2026-09-28, from a second AI he agreed with)
The screenshots ARE the product, not concept art. The next step is not "make it prettier" but "make the Hub function
like a professional AI organization while keeping this visual world": the visual operating system for the AI team.
- Don't change the existing architecture/design unless there's a real functional reason. Re-seating agents to the
  new floor split (upstairs = Research Lead, QA, Storm Watch; downstairs = Builder, Claude Code, Designer, Engine
  Mechanic, Chat Reader, Cowork, Right Hand; then YOU) is a functional reason; rebuilding the rooms is not.
- A glanceable RIGHT NOW list, one line per agent with a status color + verb (Builder - Building, Research Lead -
  Researching, QA - Waiting, Designer - Designing, Right Hand - Needs Kenny), matched by what you SEE happening in
  the room.
- The research question for every reference: "What can AI Hub steal from this to make its own system better?"

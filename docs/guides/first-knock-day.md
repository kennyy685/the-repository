# First knock day - FilthE (EN)

One screen. Print it or read it before you go.

## Before you leave
- **Bring:** phone (charged), business cards, a notepad or the app open to Today's knock, water,
  sunscreen/layers for weather, the cancel-notice/contract forms in the truck (not in your hand at
  the first knock - only once someone says yes to an inspection).
- **Wear:** clean, tucked-in, presentable - like a job interview, not a work site.
- **Area:** use the app's Today's knock walk - it's already the hottest zone, houses only, in order.
  Don't wander to "The Edge" or job-site neighbors - that's a side note, not the plan.
- **Time:** aim for 2-3 hours. Best windows: weekdays 4-7 PM (people are home from work), Saturday
  10 AM-2 PM. Nov-Feb: 3:30-5:30 PM (per the app's seasonal rule).

## At the door (say this first, every time - it's the law, 69-1602)
1. Your name.
2. "I'm with HMP Siding & Roofing."
3. What you sell - one line: "We're checking roofs in the area for storm damage" (or "we help
   homeowners update old siding and roofs" for non-storm doors).
Then ask, don't pitch: "Have you noticed any hail damage, or had anyone come look at your roof?"

## What you're allowed to say / not say
- OK: free inspection, what storm hit the area and when, what to look for, that you'll help
  document damage and meet the adjuster.
- **Never:** don't say insurance will pay, don't offer to cover/waive/lower their deductible, don't
  promise a specific price before an inspection, don't say you negotiate the claim for them.
- If they say yes to an inspection: get name, best phone, best day/time. Log it right away (tap or
  say it into the app) - don't wait until end of day, you will forget details.

## After 20 "no's" in a row
This is normal, not failure. Historically **only about 1 in 100-200 doors** turns into a booked
inspection when you're brand new (it gets better with practice - experienced reps get closer to 1 in
30). 20 no's is a normal morning, not a bad sign. Keep your pace, keep the same opening line, don't
get quieter or apologize more. Take a 5-minute water break every hour regardless of how it's going.

## Safety
- Trust your gut - skip a house that feels wrong (dogs loose, "no soliciting" posted and strictly
  enforced by the town, anyone hostile). No soliciting signs: check the town's door-to-door permit
  rules FilthE already has: some towns require you to leave if asked.
- Tell someone (FilthE's family, or log it in the app) roughly where you're walking and when you
  expect to finish.
- Daylight only for a first day. Don't knock after dark.

## When to stop for the day
- Hit your area's full walk list, OR
- 3 hours in, whichever comes first for day one. Build up from there.
- Always log before you stop: how many doors, how many talked, how many interested/booked, one
  thing that felt slow or confusing. The app (or FilthE's own notes) needs this to get better.

## Log every door (one tap or one sentence)
Not home / No / Interested / Booked. If Interested or Booked, add the address, name, and what they
said about damage, out loud into the app or written down - don't rely on memory past the next house.

# Primer dia tocando puertas - version corta en espanol (para el jefe)

**Antes de salir:** telefono cargado, tarjetas, agua. Usa la ruta del dia en la app - ya es la zona
mas caliente, solo casas, en orden.

**En la puerta (di esto primero, siempre - es la ley):**
1. Tu nombre. 2. "Soy de HMP Siding & Roofing." 3. Que vendes, una linea: "Estamos revisando techos
por danos de granizo en la zona."

**Nunca digas:** que el seguro va a pagar seguro, que cubres o bajas el deducible, un precio exacto
sin inspeccion, que tu negocias el reclamo por ellos.

**Si dicen que si a una inspeccion:** nombre, mejor telefono, mejor dia/hora. Anotalo de inmediato.

**Despues de 20 "no's" seguidos:** es normal, no un fracaso. Al principio, como 1 de cada 100-200
puertas termina en una inspeccion agendada - mejora con la practica. Sigue tu ritmo.

**Seguridad:** si algo se siente mal, saltate esa casa. Sin tocar de noche el primer dia.

**Cuando parar:** termina tu lista del dia, o 3 horas, lo que llegue primero el primer dia. Anota
antes de parar: cuantas puertas, cuantas hablaron, cuantas interesadas/agendadas.

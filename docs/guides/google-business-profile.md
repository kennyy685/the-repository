# Google Business Profile for HMP: click by click (T41)

**Why:** when a homeowner googles "HMP Siding" mid-conversation, a real profile with reviews and job photos
says "real local company". It's free. Takes about 20 minutes, plus a few days for Google to verify.

## 1. Create it (on your phone or computer)
1. Go to **business.google.com** and sign in with the Google account HMP will keep (not a throwaway).
2. Tap **Add your business** (or "Manage now").
3. **Business name:** `HMP Siding & Roofing LLC` (exactly as registered; don't add keywords, Google suspends that).
4. **Category:** type `Siding contractor` and pick it. You'll add `Roofing contractor` later (step 3).
5. **Do you want to add a location customers can visit?** → **No** (you go to them; this hides your address).
6. **Service areas:** Fremont, Omaha, Lincoln, Columbus, Blair, Wahoo, Norfolk (add or remove any).
7. **Phone:** `402-889-3385` (the office line on the logo). **Website:** skip for now.
8. Finish, then **Verify**: Google picks the method. It's often a **short video**: show the logo/sign,
   the truck or tools, and a job. Follow the prompts; it can take a few days.

## 2. Fill it out (after verification)
- **Add categories:** Roofing contractor, Gutter installer (only what you really do).
- **Hours:** your real hours (e.g. Mon-Sat 7 AM-7 PM).
- **Logo:** the H.M.P logo. **Cover photo:** your best finished job (The Edge Apartments siding).
- **Photos:** 5-10 real job photos to start, then 2-3 a week (before/after works best).
- **Services:** Siding installation, Siding repair, Roof replacement, Roof repair, Soffit and fascia,
  Flashing, Free roof and siding inspection, Storm damage inspection.
- **Description** (copy and paste, 750 characters max):

> HMP Siding & Roofing LLC is a Fremont, Nebraska siding and roofing contractor for homes and commercial
> buildings. Our crews install lap siding (including James Hardie), soffit, fascia, flashing and roofing, and
> we've worked on apartment complexes across the Fremont and Omaha area. We offer free roof and siding
> inspections with photos of anything we find, and clear written estimates. Registered and insured. Hablamos
> español - we're happy to work with you in English or Spanish.

## 3. Get the first reviews
1. In the profile, tap **Ask for reviews** → copy the short link.
2. Send it to real customers only: VTR Contracting, Nastase Contracting, The Edge property manager, and every
   job you finish from now on (same day, while they're happy).
3. Message you can send (EN): *"Thanks for working with HMP! If you have a minute, a quick Google review
   helps our small company a lot: [link]"*
   (ES): *"¡Gracias por trabajar con HMP! Si tiene un minuto, una reseña en Google nos ayuda mucho: [link]"*
4. **Rules (FTC):** never pay, discount or give gifts for reviews; never write reviews yourself or have
   family/friends write fake ones; don't only ask the happy ones. Reply to every review, good or bad.

## 4. Keep it alive (5 minutes a week)
- Post 1 update a week: a job photo + one line ("New Hardie siding in Fremont this week").
- Answer questions and messages the same day.
- Never mention covering or waiving deductibles, or promise insurance will pay (Nebraska 44-8604).

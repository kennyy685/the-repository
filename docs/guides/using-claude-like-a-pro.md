# Using Claude like a pro (2026-09-26)

For FilthE. 13 skills, short, one roofing example each. Try one a day, not all at once.

## 1. Say the goal, not the steps
Claude figures out the how. Just tell it what "done" looks like.
> "Make it so a homeowner can see their claim status on their phone without calling me."

## 2. Ask for a plan first on anything big
Before a big change, say "plan mode" or just "don't build yet, tell me the plan first." You read the plan, say yes/no,
then it builds. Skip this for tiny asks (fix a typo, change a color).
> "Plan mode: I want the app to text the homeowner 2 reminders before inspection day. What would you build? Don't code yet."

## 3. Ask for 2-3 options, not one
> "Give me 3 ways to show today's walk on the home screen. Don't build any yet - just describe them."

## 4. Send a screenshot, don't just describe
Take a screenshot on your phone, paste it into the chat. "Make it look like this" or "this button is too small" works
way better than typing a description.

## 5. Voice works
Talk instead of typing when your hands are busy - in the car, on a ladder break. Just say what you want out loud (the
app or your phone's dictation types it for you).
> "Hey the leads tab feels cluttered, can you clean it up so the hottest lead is at the top"

## 6. Say when something's wrong right away
Don't wait. The moment something looks off, say so - it costs nothing to redirect early, and a lot more to fix later.
> "No, stop - that's not what I meant. I wanted the price range to update automatically, not stay stuck."

## 7. Start a new chat for a new topic
If you're switching from "fix the app" to "write me door-hanger copy," start fresh instead of piling on. It's faster and
cheaper, and Claude won't drag in leftover context that doesn't apply.

## 8. Use short commands for repeat jobs
`/door-list Tuesday Fremont` or `/call-list 10` do a specific job every time, the same way, without you writing a
sentence. Ask "what commands do we have?" if you forget.

## 9. Correct twice, then restart
If you correct the same mistake twice and it's still wrong, don't keep trying - say "start over, here's what I actually
want" in a clean chat. Third time rarely works better; a fresh start usually does.

## 10. Ask it to interview you for big features
For anything with a lot of moving parts (like the whole "sale guide" project), let Claude ask you questions first instead
of you trying to explain everything up front.
> "I want the app to walk me through a whole claim from first knock to getting paid. Interview me about it - ask what you
> need to know - then write the plan."

## 11. Ask "why" questions any time
You can ask Claude to explain anything it built or any part of the app, in plain English, any time - no different from
asking a person.
> "Why does the quick estimate use a range instead of one number?"

## 12. One thing at a time when it's high-stakes
For legal text (the contract, the cancel notice, the deductible notice), ask Claude to double-check the exact wording
against the real law before you use it - and say so explicitly.
> "Before I print this, check that the cancel notice text matches the actual Nebraska statute word for word."

## 13. Ask what to try next
When you don't know what to ask for, just say that.
> "I don't know what the app is missing. Look at how other roofing apps compare and tell me what we should copy."

Title 210 - Nebraska Department of Insurance
Chapter 60 - Unfair Property and Casualty Settlement Practices Rule
Adopted under the Unfair Insurance Claims Settlement Practices Act, Neb. Rev. Stat. 44-1536 to 44-1544.
Effective September 1, 1992 (still in force; confirmed against current Cornell LII / Justia indexes, 2026-09-28).
Source: doi.nebraska.gov, direct PDF fetch (docs/research round 59 follow-up), https://doi.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/Chapter%2060.pdf

Selected sections relevant to notice-of-loss / claim-reporting deadlines (full chapter also covers
documentation, misrepresentation, settlement timelines, etc. - excerpted here for the notice-deadline
question; read the source PDF for the complete rule):

003.09 "Notification of claim" means any notification by a claimant, whether in writing or by other means
acceptable under the terms of an insurance policy, business custom or statute, to an insurer or its agent
which reasonably apprises the insurer of the facts pertinent to a claim;

005.03 No insurer shall deny a claim based upon the failure of a first party claimant to give written notice
of loss within a specific time limit unless the written notice requirement is a written policy condition, or
the first party claimant's failure to give written notice after being requested to do so is so unreasonable
as to constitute a breach of the claimant's contractual duty to cooperate with the insurer.

006.01 Every insurer, upon receiving notification of a claim shall, within fifteen (15) days, acknowledge
the receipt of such notice unless payment is made within that period of time. ...

006.04 Every insurer, upon receiving notification of claim, shall provide, within fifteen (15) days, the
necessary claim forms, instructions and reasonable assistance so that claimants can comply with the
insurer's reasonable requirements and policy conditions. ...

007. Standards for the prompt investigation of claims. Every insurer shall, within fifteen (15) days of
notification of claim, initiate investigation of any claim presented by a claimant.

008.01 Within fifteen (15) days after receipt by the insurer of settlement information or a properly
executed proof of loss, the claimant shall be advised of the acceptance or denial of the claim by the
insurer. No insurer shall deny a claim on the grounds of a specific policy provision, condition or exclusion
unless reference to such provision, condition, or exclusion is included in the denial. ...

Plain-English takeaway (not legal advice - confirm with an attorney/the DOI for anything specific):
- This regulation is about how NEBRASKA INSURERS must behave, not a deadline law for homeowners.
- 005.03 is the closest thing to a state rule on notice deadlines: an insurer can only deny a claim for
  late notice if (a) the policy itself actually has a written time-limit condition, or (b) the delay was
  unreasonable enough to break the homeowner's duty to cooperate. Nebraska does not set the number of
  days itself - that number, if any, lives in the policy.
- The 15-day windows in 006-008 are the INSURER's response clock (acknowledge, start investigating, accept/
  deny) once notice is given - not a homeowner filing deadline.

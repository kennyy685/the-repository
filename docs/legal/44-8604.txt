44-8604 Revised Statutes Chapter 44 44-8604 Print Friendly View Statute 44-8603 Chapter 44 Index View Statute 44-8605 Chapter 44 44-8604. Residential contractor; prohibited acts. A residential contractor shall not promise to rebate any portion of an insurance deductible as an inducement to the sale of goods or services. A promise to rebate any portion of an insurance deductible includes granting any allowance or offering any discount against the fees to be charged or paying an insured or a person directly or indirectly associated with the residential real estate any form of compensation, except for any item of nominal value.

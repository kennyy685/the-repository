Nebraska Contractor Registration Act - key sections (fetched directly from nebraskalegislature.gov, 2026-09-26)
Full act: Neb. Rev. Stat. 48-2101 to 48-2117.

48-2103. Terms, defined. "Contractor" = any individual/firm/LLC/corp/association doing construction
(building, altering, repairing, dismantling, demolition of real property, incl. roofing/siding-type work).
Contractor ALSO includes any subcontractor, and any person providing or arranging labor for such activity,
whether as employee or independent contractor.

48-2104. Registration required. (1) Before performing any construction work in Nebraska, a contractor
shall be registered with the Department of Labor. A contractor doing business under more than one name
needs a registration number per name. Exemptions: working on your own property, or earning under $5,000/
year in construction services.

48-2105. Application contents: legal name + FEIN/SSN, Nebraska principal place of business (or address +
NE registered agent if out of state), NE phone number, entity type, sales/use tax election, officer/owner
names, PROOF OF WORKERS' COMP INSURANCE (or self-insurance certificate, or signed statement it isn't
required), and a description of the business/SIC code. Renews annually with the fee (48-2107, up to $40).

48-2108. Registration number format: a 5-digit number + 2-digit year of issuance (e.g. 12345-26).
NOTE: nowhere in 48-2101 to 48-2117 does the Act require the registration number to be printed on
contracts, ads, vehicles, or signage -- that requirement does not exist in this Act (only the deductible
notice under 44-8607 and the cancel-right notices under 69-1601/1604 are statutorily required print text).
Printing the reg# anyway (current HMP practice, per CLAUDE.md T64) is good practice, not a legal must.

48-2110/2111. Registration is revoked if workers' comp coverage lapses; notice by certified mail; a
revoked registration cannot be reinstated, contractor must reapply for a new number.

48-2114. Violation; penalty. Commissioner issues a citation for (a) not being registered or (b)
registration info not substantially complete/accurate. Administrative penalty: up to $500 for a first
violation, up to $5,000 for a second or later violation (same $500/$5,000 structure as the Employee
Classification Act, 48-2907, verified round 35). 15 working days to contest. NOTE: subsection (4)'s
60-working-day grace period to register penalty-free after a first citation "shall remain in effect until
March 1, 2009" -- that grace period is long expired; a first-time citation today can draw the up-to-$500
penalty immediately, no automatic grace window.

48-2117. Department of Labor publishes a public database of registered contractors (workers' comp status
per contractor). A contractor is removed from the database for non-compliance.

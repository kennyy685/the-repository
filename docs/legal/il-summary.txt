1. Deductible waiver/rebate - 815 ILCS 513/18 (Home Repair and Remodeling Act)
   Paraphrase: "A contractor offering home repair or remodeling services shall not advertise or
   promise to pay or rebate all or any portion of any insurance deductible as an inducement to the
   sale of goods or services." Definition of "promise to pay or rebate" mirrors Nebraska's 44-8604
   (any allowance, discount, or compensation to the insured or an associated person).
   Source: ilga.gov 815 ILCS 513/18 (via search summary); FindLaw 815-513-18.

2. Home-solicitation cancel right - 815 ILCS 505/2B (Consumer Fraud Act) + Illinois AG guidance
   Paraphrase: 3 business days to cancel a door-to-door / home-repair sale of $25+; the 3-day
   period does not start until the buyer is given a "Notice of Cancellation" with the seller's
   address. Extended to 15 full business days if the buyer is 65 or older. No storm-specific
   extension found.
   Source: Illinois Attorney General "Three-Day Right to Cancel" consumer guide; FindLaw 815-505-2b.

3. Contractor registration/licensing - 225 ILCS 335 (Illinois Roofing Industry Licensing Act)
   Illinois is one of the minority of states that DOES require a state ROOFING LICENSE (not just
   registration) from the Dept. of Financial and Professional Regulation (IDFPR), with an exam,
   liability + workers'-comp insurance, and a bond. Two classes: Residential (Limited) and
   Residential/Commercial/Industrial (Unlimited). This is stricter than Nebraska's registration-only
   model - use "licensed" only for Illinois, never carry that word over to Nebraska copy.
   Source: 225 ILCS 335 (via search summary); ilroofinginstitute.com; trentcotney.com.

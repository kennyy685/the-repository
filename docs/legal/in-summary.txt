1. Deductible waiver/rebate - IC 24-5-11-10.5 (Home Improvement Contracts Act / "HICA")
   IMPORTANT: an earlier search pass returned a different, likely-wrong cite ("IC 27-1-15.8-4",
   framed as insurance fraud). A follow-up, more targeted search consistently landed on
   IC 24-5-11-10.5, titled "Prohibited Acts; Contracts With Insured Consumers for Exterior
   Improvements; Right to Cancel; Services Performed; Acting as Public Adjuster Prohibited" - this
   is the working cite, corroborated by Justia, FindLaw and LawServer all agreeing on the section
   number and title.
   Paraphrase: a "real property improvement supplier" shall not advertise, offer, or promise to pay
   or rebate any part of an insurance deductible to induce a person to enter into a contract, nor
   grant an allowance/discount against the price, nor pay compensation (gifts, prizes, bonuses,
   coupons, credits, referral fees). SAME SECTION also says the supplier shall not act as a public
   adjuster - Indiana folds its public-adjuster-limit language into this same statute, useful for
   the legal-pack's public-adjuster field.
   Source: law.justia.com IC 24-5-11-10.5; FindLaw IN 24-5-11-10.5; lawserver.com.

2. Home-solicitation cancel right - IC 24-5-10-9 (Home Solicitation Sales, Ch. 10) - note the
   original guess of "IC 24-4.5" was wrong; the real chapter is IC 24-5-10, with a parallel/updated
   chapter IC 24-5-11-10.6 for contracts entered after 6/30/2017 specifically for home-improvement
   contracts.
   Paraphrase: buyer may cancel before midnight of the 3rd business day after the parties finally
   agree; "business day" excludes Sunday and legal holidays. Two copies of a written cancellation
   notice (10-point boldface, on a separate document) are required. Violation is a Class C
   infraction and a deceptive act actionable by the AG or the consumer.
   Source: law.justia.com IC 24-5-10-9; theindianalawyer.com "cooling-off periods".

3. Contractor registration/licensing - NO state-level trade license for roofers (or general
   contractors) in Indiana; contractors register with the Indiana Secretary of State (standard
   business registration, not trade-specific). Cities/counties (Indianapolis, Fort Wayne, Evansville,
   South Bend) run their own local licensing programs. IC 24-5-11 (HICA) imposes written-contract
   rules on residential home-improvement work over $150 regardless of local licensing.
   Source: indianaroofauthority.com; adaptdigitalsolutions.com Indiana contractor guide.

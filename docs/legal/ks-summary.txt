1. Deductible waiver/rebate - K.S.A. 50-6,132 / 50-6,133 (Kansas Residential Roofing Practices Act,
   from 2011 HB 2307)
   Paraphrase: "A residential roofing contractor providing roofing goods and services to be paid by
   an insured from the proceeds of a property or casualty insurance policy shall not advertise or
   promise to pay or rebate all or any part of an applicable insurance deductible." Same "allowance/
   discount/compensation" definition pattern as Nebraska's 44-8604.
   Source: ksrevisor.gov 50-6,132 / 50-6,133 (via search summary); Rhoden Roofing, Roofing Contractor
   trade-press summaries.

2. Home-solicitation cancel right - K.S.A. 50-640 (Kansas Consumer Protection Act, "Door-to-door
   sales; cancellation; required disclosures; notice of cancellation")
   Paraphrase: buyer may cancel by midnight of the 3rd business day after signing a door-to-door
   sale of $25+; seller must disclose orally AND in writing and give a duplicate Notice of
   Cancellation form. No storm-specific extension found.
   Source: FindLaw KS 50-640; Douglas County KS DA FAQ; KS AG "Your Home and Car" consumer page.

3. Contractor registration/licensing - Kansas Roofing Registration Act (KRRA), K.S.A. 50-6,122 to
   50-6,128 (2013). Roofing contractors must get a REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE from the Kansas
   Attorney General's Roofing Registration Unit (not a trade license) - certificate of liability +
   workers'-comp insurance, $125-250 fee. Registration number must be given to any permit-issuing
   official. This is the closest match to Nebraska's model - use "registered," not "licensed," for
   Kansas roofing contractors too.
   Source: KS AG "Roofing Registration" page; kansasroofingassociation.org.

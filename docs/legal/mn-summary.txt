1. Deductible waiver/rebate - Minn. Stat. Sec. 325E.66 (Trade Practices)
   Paraphrase: "Residential contractors providing residential roofing goods and services to be paid
   by an insured from the proceeds of a property or casualty insurance policy shall not advertise or
   promise to pay or rebate all or part of any applicable insurance deductible." Contractors must
   include a written notice of this rule in their initial estimate. If violated, the insurer need
   not consider that contractor's estimate; the insured or insurer may sue for damages. Same
   enforcement pattern as Colorado's 6-22-105 and Nebraska's 44-8604.
   Source: law.justia.com Minn. Stat. 325E.66 (2010 text, likely still current - re-verify); MN Dept.
   of Labor and Industry "Contractors and insurance deductibles" page; mplsroofing.com.

2. Home-solicitation cancel right - Minn. Stat. Sec. 325G.06 (and 325G.07/.08 for notice/writing
   requirements)
   Paraphrase: buyer may cancel by midnight of the 3rd business day after the sale; cancellation by
   mail is effective on deposit; seller who performed work before cancellation gets NO compensation
   (same rule as Nebraska's 69-1606(5)). "Home solicitation sale" definition matches Nebraska's
   69-1601 closely (personal solicitation away from seller's place of business, $25+ purchase price).
   No storm-specific extension found.
   Source: revisor.mn.gov 325G.06 (via search summary, page itself not opened); madgettlaw.com legal
   blog on the same section.

3. Contractor registration/licensing - Minnesota REQUIRES A STATE LICENSE (not just registration)
   for residential roofers: "Residential Roofer," "Residential Building Contractor," or "Residential
   Remodeler" license from the Dept. of Labor and Industry (DLI), including an exam for
   employees/qualifying persons. Subcontractor roofers need only register (not license) through
   DLI's Construction Contractor Registration Program. Contractors under $15,000/yr gross residential
   receipts are exempt but need a Certificate of Exemption. Minnesota is stricter than Nebraska here
   - use "licensed" for MN prime roofing contractors, "registered" for MN subcontractors.
   Source: MN DLI "Residential contractors, remodelers, roofers" page; dli.mn.gov roofer-license page.

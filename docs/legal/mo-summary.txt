1. Deductible waiver/rebate - RSMo Sec. 407.725 (Merchandising Practices Act)
   Paraphrase: near-identical wording to Nebraska's 44-8604 - "a contractor shall not advertise or
   promise to pay or rebate all or any portion of any insurance deductible as an inducement to the
   sale of goods or services," with the same expanded definition (allowance, discount, compensation,
   gift, prize, bonus, coupon, credit, referral fee). SAME SECTION also bars a contractor from
   representing/negotiating an insurance claim on the owner's behalf for roof or exterior repair
   work - i.e. Missouri's public-adjuster-style claims-negotiation ban is folded into this same
   statute, not a separate insurance-code section. Useful: this doubles as the public-adjuster-limit
   citation for Missouri.
   Source: law.justia.com RSMo 407.725; carnahanevans.com "New Law Governing Roof & Exterior Repair".

2. Home-solicitation cancel right - RSMo Sec. 407.705 (and 407.700-.720 series)
   Paraphrase: buyer may cancel by midnight of the 3rd business day after signing; "business day"
   excludes Saturday, Sunday, legal holidays. Cancellation by mailed notice is effective on deposit.
   No storm-specific extension found.
   Source: law.justia.com RSMo 407.705; FindLaw RSMo 407.710.

3. Contractor registration/licensing - Missouri Senate Bill 326, effective 1/1/2023: STATEWIDE
   REGISTRATION (not a trade license) with the Dept. of Commerce and Insurance, requiring $1M
   liability insurance, vehicle insurance, workers'-comp. No statewide roofing license; cities/
   counties layer on their own local licenses. Use "registered," not "licensed," for the statewide
   piece, but check local-city wording since some Missouri cities may use "licensed" for their own
   permit.
   Source: freedomroofingmo.com; missouriroofauthority.com; frgwillhelp.com Missouri roofing guides.

4. Public adjuster / claims negotiation - see item 1 above; RSMo 407.725 itself appears to bar a
   contractor from negotiating an owner's insurance claim for roof/exterior work. UNVERIFIED against
   primary text; the exact scope (does it also require a public-adjuster license generally, or just
   bar contractors specifically) needs the real statute text.
5. Assignment of benefits - not independently checked. UNVERIFIED.
6. Door-to-door permit - no statewide permit statute found beyond the 407.705 cancel-right rule;
   local permits vary by city.

1. Deductible waiver/rebate - 59 O.S. Sec. 1151.30 (Oklahoma Roofing Contractor Registration Act,
   from HB 1940, effective 11/1/2022)
   IMPORTANT: an earlier search pass returned a different, likely-wrong cite ("Title 36 Sec.
   3614.1" / Insurance Code). A follow-up, more targeted search consistently landed on 59 O.S.
   1151.30, inside the same Title 59 Roofing Contractor Registration Act as the registration
   requirement below (Justia's own section page). Treat 59 O.S. 1151.30 as the working cite but
   flag it for direct confirmation - two different secondary sources disagreed on the Title number.
   Paraphrase: a roofing contractor paid from insurance proceeds shall not, as an inducement,
   "advertise or promise to pay, directly or indirectly, all or part of any applicable insurance
   deductible or offer to compensate an insured for providing any service." If violated, the
   insurer need not consider that contractor's estimate; contractors/adjusters/insurers must give
   written notice of the law with any estimate.
   Source: law.justia.com OK Title 59 Sec. 1151.30; roofingcontractor.com; whirlwindroofing.com.

2. Home-solicitation cancel right - 14A O.S. Sec. 2-502 (Oklahoma Consumer Credit Code, NOT Title 15)
   Paraphrase: buyer may cancel a home solicitation sale by midnight of the 3rd business day after
   signing. Notice of cancellation need not be in any particular form; mailed notice is effective
   on deposit. No emergency-services exception carve-out found beyond the standard one. No
   storm-specific extension found.
   Source: law.justia.com OK Title 14A Sec. 2-502.

3. Contractor registration/licensing - Oklahoma Roofing Contractor Registration Act (RCRA), 59 O.S.
   Sec. 1151.1 to 1151.x, administered by the Construction Industries Board (CIB), effective 2010.
   REGISTRATION (not a trade license/exam) required before advertising or acting as a roofing
   contractor; liability insurance of $500,000+, workers'-comp proof. Failure to register is a
   misdemeanor (up to $500/violation). Use "registered," not "licensed," for Oklahoma too - same
   pattern as Nebraska.
   Source: oklahoma.gov CIB "Active Roofing Contractor Requirements"; law.justia.com Title 59
   Sec. 1151.4/1151.5.

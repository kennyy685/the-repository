1. Deductible waiver/rebate - Texas Insurance Code Sec. 707.002 (added by HB 2102, 2019, effective
   9/1/2019)
   Paraphrase: it is a violation for a contractor to "pay for, waive, absorb, rebate, credit or
   offset, or otherwise assist the insured in any other manner in avoiding monetary payment of the
   required insurance deductible applicable to the insurance claim for repairs to the roof." A
   Class B misdemeanor (up to $2,000 fine / 180 days). Contracts of $1,000+ tied to an insurance
   settlement must carry a written notice that the policyholder is responsible for the deductible.
   Source: multiple contractor/insurance-law blogs citing Tex. Ins. Code 707.002; capitol.texas.gov
   HB 2102 bill text (not opened directly).

2. Home-solicitation cancel right - Texas Business & Commerce Code Chapter 601 ("Cancellation of
   Certain Consumer Transactions"), Subchapter B, esp. Sec. 601.052-.053
   Paraphrase: buyer may cancel by midnight of the 3rd business day after signing, for a sale
   solicited away from the seller's place of business, $25+ goods/services or $100+ real estate.
   Seller must disclose orally AND in writing, and attach a duplicate Notice of Cancellation form.
   No storm-specific extension found for this chapter; Texas has a SEPARATE, roofing-specific
   claim-related rule under Ins. Code 707 above.
   Source: Texas Law Help "3-Day Right to Cancel"; Texas AG consumer page; FindLaw Bus&Com 601.053.

3. Contractor registration/licensing - NO statewide roofing license today. TDLR does not regulate
   roofing contractors as of this round; a voluntary RCAT (Roofing Contractors Association of Texas)
   credential exists. NOTE: Texas HB 3344 creates a new "Reroofing Contractor" regulatory framework
   under TDLR, nominally effective 9/1/2025, but the search summary says the advisory board was
   still being set up - CONFIRM current status before telling FilthE Texas needs no registration;
   this may have changed since. Many Texas cities (Houston, San Antonio, Austin, Dallas) require
   local registration/permits regardless of state law.
   Source: servicetitan.com Texas roofing guide; proroofersofconroe.com "New Texas Reroofing License".

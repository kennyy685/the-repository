# O7 · Sale Guide: the app as the salesman's right hand (DRAFT, FilthE 2026-09-26)

FilthE: "The app should be the salesman's right hand: it guides him through the sale and assists through it,
leading, guiding, asking for info and telling him what to ask for. Like a construction worker's hammer."

## The idea in one line
Every lead has a **"Next step" card** that says what to do right now: what to say, what to ask, what to collect,
and what the law requires. It moves to the next card when the step is done. No menus to hunt through.

## The steps (one card each)
Two paths share the same start: **Insurance** (storm damage) and **Cash** (old house). Every card is EN/ES.

| # | Step | What the card does | Collects |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **At the door** | 1-line opener for this house (storm date + hail size, or house age); the top 3 objections for this kind of house with short answers | Result tap; first name; best time |
| 2 | **Inspection set** | Confirms day/time; sends the reminder text (template) | Date/time, phone (asked, not bought) |
| 3 | **Inspection** | Step-by-step photo checklist (roof + siding), with a safety line; "prove the mismatch" for siding | Photos, test-square counts, damage notes |
| 4 | **Talk at the table** | Insurance: plain explanation of how a claim works, the deductible is always theirs, *they* file the claim. Cash: Good/Better/Best (Vinyl vs Hardie) with the quick estimate | Chosen option, questions asked |
| 5 | **Paperwork** | The written contract (never a handshake), 3-day cancel explained out loud, deductible notice (insurance), 2 cancel copies; a checklist that must be complete before "Signed" | Signed contract photo, cancel-by date |
| 6a | **Claim (insurance)** | Homeowner files → claim # → adjuster meeting prep (checklist) → scope in → supplements → checks (ACV, mortgage, depreciation) | Claim # / insurer / adjuster date / scope |
| 6b | **Measure + order (cash)** | Measurements → material order list → deposit | Measurements, deposit |
| 7 | **Build** | Crew + date, day-of checklist, completion photos | Photos, completion date |
| 8 | **Get paid + review** | Final invoice/checks, completion certificate, review request text, "who else on your street?" | Paid, review link sent |

## What the app asks for (and never)
- It asks for the next missing fact at the right moment ("Ask for the claim number when they get it").
- It never suggests covering, waiving or rebating a deductible (Nebraska 44-8604), promising insurance pays, or
  negotiating the claim. It says "registered", never "licensed".
- It never asks FilthE to type what it can pick up from a tap or a photo.

## Built from what exists
Leads + stages, Today's knock, quick estimate, photo checklist, claims, follow-up texts, Practice Door objections,
contract drafts, cancel notice, adjuster checklist, claims-101. O7 puts them in order behind one "Next step" card.

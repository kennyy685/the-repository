# What HMP's job contract must contain (T38 research, 2026-09-25)

**Status: from search summaries of official pages (the .gov pages were blocked here). Have a person open the
links, and a Nebraska attorney review the template, before the first signature.**

1. **Insurance-paid jobs, Nebraska Insured Homeowners Protection Act (44-8601..8608)**
   - Cancel right (44-8603): until midnight of the later of (a) the 3rd business day after signing, or (b) the 3rd
     business day after the insurer's written notice that the claim isn't covered. Written notice to the business
     address on the contract; effective when mailed. Refund within 10 days (minus the reasonable value of
     homeowner-approved emergency work, like tarping).
   - Deductible notice (44-8607), CAPITALIZED 14-POINT TYPE, signed by the insured: "IT IS A VIOLATION OF THE INSURANCE
     LAWS OF NEBRASKA TO REBATE ANY PORTION OF AN INSURANCE DEDUCTIBLE AS AN INDUCEMENT TO THE INSURED TO ACCEPT A
     RESIDENTIAL CONTRACTOR'S PROPOSAL TO REPAIR DAMAGED PROPERTY. REBATE OF A DEDUCTIBLE INCLUDES GRANTING ANY
     ALLOWANCE OR OFFERING ANY DISCOUNT AGAINST THE FEES TO BE CHARGED FOR WORK TO BE PERFORMED OR PAYING THE INSURED
     HOMEOWNER THE DEDUCTIBLE AMOUNT SET FORTH IN THE INSURANCE POLICY."
   - Avoid "assignment of benefits" (44-8605) and anything that acts on the claim.
2. **All door-to-door sales, FTC Cooling-Off Rule (16 CFR 429):** 3 business days; give 2 copies of a completed
   "Notice of Cancellation" form, plus the contract with the date, seller name/address and "You, the buyer, may cancel
   this transaction at any time prior to midnight of the third business day after the date of this transaction."
   Tell the buyer out loud. Applies to $25+ at the buyer's home.
3. **Nebraska Home Solicitation Sales Act (69-1601..1607):** 3 business days; a conspicuous "BUYER'S RIGHT TO CANCEL"
   caption; refund within 10 days. One combined cancellation form can satisfy #2 and #4.
4. **Contractor Registration Act:** registration is required; whether the # must print on contracts wasn't
   confirmed, so print it anyway.
5. **Standard parts:** scope by trade/area; price (insurance jobs: "the insurance-approved amount" + the deductible
   notice); a payment schedule by milestone; materials; warranty; signed change orders; permits; cleanup.

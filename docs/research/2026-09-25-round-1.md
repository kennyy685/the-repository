# Research round 1 (2026-09-25): blind spots, door-knocking, subbing + claims

**Bottom line: the bottleneck is knocking, not software.** The engine, maps and lists are ahead of most
first-year competitors; zero doors are logged. Every idea below is about turning what exists into
inspections and signed jobs. Rough industry numbers (blog estimates, not audited): about 1-3 signed
jobs per 100 doors in a storm week; knock within 24-72 hours of hail, when most leads are won.

Method note: this cloud session could search the web but not open pages (egress blocked), so sources
are search-result based. Verify legal specifics against nebraskalegislature.gov before they go into a
contract; one scout's claim of a "5 business days" insurer-disclosure deadline is NOT verified.

## Ranked ideas (payoff for effort)
1. **Knock now, track simply.** 20-40 doors this week (Columbus Turf 1 first), phone notes or paper
   if needed. First 10 seconds: "I'm doing quick free inspections for homes on this block." Hit each
   street 3 times; follow up "come back later" within 24-48 hours. (FilthE, S, free)
2. **Get on 2-3 restoration companies' storm overflow lists**, Nastase first (pitch drafted). Bring the
   insurance certificate and crew photos; get pay terms in writing. (FilthE, S, free)
3. **Bilingual door hanger at every no-answer door** (half or more of a block). (Code, S, $)
4. **One-page bilingual photo + adjuster-meeting checklist** for the truck: 4 slopes, soft metals,
   gutters, siding, test square, date stamps; always attend the adjuster meeting. (Code, S, free)
5. **Google Business Profile now**, so a knock isn't the first time a homeowner sees HMP; ask every
   finished job for a review. (FilthE, S, free)
6. **Send the boss to Spanish-speaking blocks** (Fremont, Schuyler, Columbus, Lexington, Crete) with
   Spanish leave-behinds; one signed job seeds referrals on the block. (FilthE + boss, S, free)
7. **Cash-flow rule for direct jobs:** don't start until the first insurance check (ACV) is in hand;
   keep a materials reserve; depreciation is paid after completion, often 60-120 days. (FilthE, S)
8. **Contract language:** 3-day cancel right (44-8603) and deductible notice; never rebate/waive a
   deductible (44-8604); never negotiate claims (public adjuster license). (Code template, S)
9. **Supplements** only as photo- and code-backed scope revisions (drip edge, ice-and-water, flashing),
   never negotiation. (Later, M)

## Watch out
- More engine features can replace selling. Freeze big builds until doors are being logged.
- Don't over-pitch the first knock; skip "No Soliciting" doors; never say insurance will cover it.

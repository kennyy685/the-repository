# Research round 4 (2026-09-25): make the HMP App better and sellable

**Bottom line:** HMP's edge over the $150-600/month app stacks roofers pay for is real: **Spanish built in, one
tool at one price, storm + old-house leads together, and an AI you just talk to.** None of the 9 competitors
checked offer bilingual or a conversational assistant. But prove it on HMP's own jobs first (6-12 months);
every successful roofing app (Roofr, JobNimbus, Trussi) was used in-house before it was sold.

## What to build next (better for HMP, sellable later)
1. **Follow-up nag:** any lead with no contact in 24h shows red on Today (the first responder wins ~81%). (Builder, S)
2. **Before/after photos + review request** in one tap at job completion. (Builder, S)
3. **Supplement nag** in Money: "3 supplements pending >14 days". (Builder, S)
4. **Staged payments** for cash jobs: deposit -> progress -> final, flag >30 days overdue. (Builder, M)
5. **Cheap now, costly later:** a `company_id` on every app record + per-company config; NOAA/Census credit lines on pages. (Builder + Engine, S)
6. **Don't build yet:** logins for other companies, billing, onboarding. Not until customer #1.

## Decisions for FilthE + the boss (later)
- Who owns the software: HMP LLC or FilthE? Settle before anyone pays.
- Price idea when ready: $99-199/month per company, up to 5 users.
- First customers: warm network (Nastase, VTR), NE/IA roofing Facebook groups; a free 60-day pilot for a testimonial.

## Scout: What small roofers need (round 4, 2026-09-25)
1. Speed-to-lead + "no contact in 24h" nag. First responder wins ~81%; automated follow-up closes 31% vs 14% manual. [jobnimbus blog; pitchit.ai]
2. One-tap before/after photos + review request at job completion. [roofingcontractor.com; builderlync.com]
3. Supplement tracker that nags ("3 supplements pending >14 days"), using the existing claims.supplements[]. [ketterly.com]
4. Bilingual, phone-only as a headline selling point (Workhand, Workiz and SolvPro market "Spanish app"). [servicetoolsguide.com]
5. Staged payments tracker (deposit -> progress -> final) with a ">30 days overdue" flag; 82% of contractors wait 30+ days. [roofingcontractor.com collecting money]
Quit reasons: crashes/lag, hidden add-on pricing, photo limits, per-user pricing. Pay: $100+/user/mo CRMs are too rich for 1-5 person shops.
Tips: text every new lead within 5 min; ask for reviews on the spot. Don't build an Xactimate supplement writer; no auto-drip to unknown numbers.

## Scout: Turning it into a product (round 4, 2026-09-25)
1. Keep it in-house until proven on HMP jobs (6-12 months). Roofr, JobNimbus and Trussi.ai were all contractor-built and used in-house first. [roofingcontractor.com]
2. Cheap now, costly later: a `company_id` on every data doc and a per-company config; NOAA/Census credit lines on pages. DON'T build auth, billing or onboarding yet.
3. Legal: NOAA/NWS data is public domain (no implied endorsement). The Census API needs a "not endorsed by the Census Bureau" line. Nebraska parcels are open; other states vary.
4. Pricing idea: $99-199/mo per company, up to 5 users (JobNimbus $225-550 + seats; AccuLynx $55-120/user).
5. First 5 customers: warm network (Nastase, VTR), NE/IA roofing Facebook groups, contractor associations; a free 60-day pilot for a testimonial. No cold texts.
6. LLC/IP talk with the boss before any money changes hands: who owns the software (HMP or FilthE)? Decision for FilthE and the boss.

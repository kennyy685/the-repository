# Follow-up texts (EN = Kenny, ES = Alex), 2026-09-26
Only send to homeowners who gave their number at the door that day. No insurance promises, no deductible talk.
Cadence: the same day within 1-2 h; reminders the day before + ~2 h before; photos after the inspection the same day or next morning; no-show: within 30-60 min, then once around day 2, then stop. Stop as soon as they reply.
Sources: repcard.com, ghostrep.ai, lumioforge.com (snippets); curogram.com, mailchimp.com SMS reminders (opened); build-folio.com.

A Interested. EN: Hi {first_name}, this is Kenny with HMP Siding & Roofing. Good talking today at {address}. Want to lock in a free inspection this week? Reply with a day that works.
  ES: Hola {first_name}, soy Alex de HMP Siding & Roofing. Un gusto hablar hoy en {address}. ¿Agendamos una inspección gratis esta semana? Dime qué día te queda bien.
B Booked. EN: You're set, {first_name}! Inspection at {address} on {time}. We'll check the roof/siding and show you photos after. Text here with any questions.
  ES: ¡Listo, {first_name}! Inspección en {address} el {time}. Revisamos techo y siding y te mostramos fotos al final. Escríbeme si tienes preguntas.
C Day before. EN: Reminder: HMP inspection tomorrow at {time}, {address}. Takes about 20-30 min. See you then!
  ES: Recordatorio: inspección de HMP mañana a las {time} en {address}. Dura unos 20-30 min. ¡Nos vemos!
D Hour before. EN: On our way, {first_name} - see you at {address} around {time}.
  ES: Vamos en camino, {first_name} - llegamos a {address} cerca de {time}.
E After inspection. EN: Thanks for today, {first_name}! Photos from {address} are ready - I'll walk you through what we found and next steps. Good time to call?
  ES: ¡Gracias por hoy, {first_name}! Ya tengo las fotos de {address}. Te explico lo que encontramos y los próximos pasos. ¿Cuándo te puedo llamar?
F Spouse. EN: No problem, {first_name}! I'll send the photos/notes from {address} so you both can look together. Happy to answer questions for either of you.
  ES: Sin problema, {first_name}. Te mando las fotos y notas de {address} para que las vean juntos. Con gusto respondo dudas a los dos.
G No-show. EN: Hi {first_name}, we're at {address} for the {time} inspection but didn't catch you - all good! Want to pick a new time?
  ES: Hola {first_name}, estamos en {address} para la inspección de las {time} pero no te encontramos - no hay problema. ¿Buscamos otro horario?
H No reply (day 2-5). EN: Hi {first_name}, just checking - still a good time to look at the roof/siding at {address}? No rush, whenever works for you.
  ES: Hola {first_name}, solo confirmando - ¿todavía es buen momento para revisar el techo o siding en {address}? Sin prisa, cuando te acomode.

The boss sets HMP's real prices (T51). The numbers come from search summaries of cost guides and Omaha contractor pages (direct fetch was blocked), so spot-check them against suppliers.

| Item | Low | Typical | High | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vinyl siding installed (incl. tear-off) | $400 | $700-900 | $1,200 | per square (100 sf); Omaha $6-11/sf |
| James Hardie fiber cement installed | $600 | $900-1,200 | $1,800 | per square; Omaha $8-14/sf |
| Architectural shingle roof, 1-layer tear-off | $350 | $450-550 | $850 | per square |

| Minimum job: siding repair | $200 | $300-400 | $454 | per job |

What raises the price: steep pitch (>6/12) or 2-3 stories (+15-35%); extra layers (disposal roughly doubles); complex roofs and tight access.
Resale value (JLC Cost vs Value 2025, West North Central): a new roof recoups ~54% of its cost; fiber-cement siding ~68-75% (the best of the siding options).

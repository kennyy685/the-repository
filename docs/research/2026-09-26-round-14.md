# Research round 14 (2026-09-26): voice-first logging + in-the-moment sales coaching

Not repeating round 9 (first-90-days coaching plan, follow-up reminders) - this round is about *how the log/coach
mechanism itself should work*, feeding into round 9's content.

## Bottom line
1. **The gap CLAUDE.md names ("123 Oak St, inspection Tuesday, hail on north slope") is a solved pattern elsewhere** -
   voice-note apps (Audionotes, SpeakNotes, VoiceToNotes) already do "speak freely -> structured note," claiming
   95%+ transcription accuracy on clear speech [source 3] - but **every one of them still shows a confirm/edit step
   before saving**, none auto-commits [source 3, 4]. Copy the confirm step, don't skip it for speed.
2. **Addresses and Spanish are the two accuracy risks**, not general dictation - no source tested address-specific or
   Spanish-dictation accuracy directly (flagged unproven this round); the safe design response is the same either way:
   **structure extraction proposes fields, human taps to confirm**, so a misheard street number gets caught before it
   becomes a wrong record, in either language.
3. **Live in-call AI coaching is real in B2B sales tools (Hyperbound's "Perform"/Live Call Coaching) but it's built on
   phone/video call audio streams and huge training data (2M+ hours) [source 2]** - not something HMP can replicate
   live, door to door, this year. What's realistic and already proven useful: **coaching timed just before or just
   after the moment**, not during it - matches round 9's end-of-day review and the field-canvassing coaching pattern.
   Gong and similar tools are fundamentally **post-call analysis**, reinforcing that "after, not during" is the norm
   even in well-funded sales tech, not a compromise HMP is settling for.
4. **SalesRabbit's own coaching (Amplify) leans on a separate recording/analysis partner (Siro), not live in-app
   prompts** [source 1] - further evidence that "coach before the door, review after," not "coach live mid-pitch," is
   the achievable and industry-normal pattern for a small team.

## Spec 1: hold-to-talk logging in the app chat (EN or ES)

**Flow**
1. FilthE holds a mic button in the King chat (already planned per CLAUDE.md) any time - walking away from a door,
   in the truck, after a call.
2. Speech-to-text runs (same webview caveat as round 13 - confirm it works on-device before relying on it; if broken,
   fall back to typed chat, which the King chat already supports).
3. Claude parses the transcript for: address, date/time mentioned, stage word ("inspection," "booked," "no answer,"
   "claim filed," insurer name, dollar figures), and free text notes - reusing the same fields the Lead Tracker /
   Claim Tracker already store (CLAUDE.md's `claims` schema, the 9-stage pipeline).
4. **One-tap confirm card appears**, not an auto-save: shows the parsed fields plainly ("123 Oak St - stage: inspection
   booked - Tuesday - note: hail on north slope") with a single **Confirm** button and an **Edit** option (opens the
   normal typed/tap fields, never a re-recording requirement). This matches every voice-note app in source 3/4: parse,
   then let the human check before it's final.
5. If the address doesn't match anything in the door lists or existing leads, the card flags it clearly ("New address -
   confirm to add") rather than silently guessing or silently dropping it.
6. Confirmed card writes to the same `leads`/`doors`/`claims` collections the rest of the app already uses - no new
   data model, just a new input path into the existing one.

**Why this matters for HMP specifically:** FilthE never types into spreadsheets (CLAUDE.md, "How FilthE works") - this
is the direct fix for lead-tool gap #4, and it's low-effort because the storage side already exists; only the
capture -> parse -> confirm path is new.

**Bilingual note:** the same flow should accept Spanish input (for when FilthE logs a conversation he just had in
Spanish, or dictates a note in whichever language is faster in the moment) - reuse round 13's glossary so terms like
"ajustador" or "deducible" spoken in a voice note map to the same stored English field names, not two different words
for the same thing across records.

## Spec 2: the 10-second "before the next door" coaching card

**What it is:** a small card shown once, right when FilthE finishes one door and the app is about to hand him the next
address (per `todaywalk`'s walking-order list) - not during the knock, not a popup mid-conversation.

**What it says (one line, picked by simple rules, not a live AI judgment call):**
- Early tenure (round 9's weeks 1-2): a reframe line - "Doors knocked today: N. That's the job, not the yeses."
- If the last door was a "no" streak (3+ in a row): a short reset line - "Long walks have runs like this. Reset,
  smile, next door." (matches round 9's finding that a streak of no's, read as failure, is the #1 early quit cause)
- If there's an unconfirmed hail-evidence note for the *next* address (hud.json's `hail_evidence`): "Porch/gutter
  photo shows hail here - lead with that."
- If it's a follow-up door, not fresh: pull the exact reason from the last visit's note ("Interested, said call after
  5pm" style) rather than a generic line.
- Once a week (not every door): a single referral-ask reminder if a job finished that week (round 9's referral spec).

**Why 10 seconds, one line, rule-based, not a live AI coach:** the coaching-tech field (Hyperbound, Gong, Siro) proves
even well-resourced tools do coaching *around* the interaction, not word-by-word during it [sources 1, 2] - a rules-
based one-liner pulled from data the app already has (walk position, last outcome, hail evidence) delivers the same
"right prompt at the right moment" value without needing a live model in the loop mid-conversation, and it can't
misfire into anything that sounds like sales pressure or a legal problem.

## Ranked build list (top 6, effort S/M/L)
1. **Confirm card for hold-to-talk logging** (Spec 1, steps 3-6) - reuses existing data collections, biggest single
   fix for CLAUDE.md's named gap #4. Who: Builder + Code. Effort: **M**.
2. **Hold-to-talk mic button in the King chat**, EN/ES, with typed-chat fallback if the webview mic doesn't work
   (shares the round-13 webview spike - do that spike once, use the answer for both features). Who: Code. Effort: **S**
   (once the spike from round 13 is done).
3. **"Before the next door" coaching card**, rule-based, wired into `todaywalk`'s walk sequence. Who: Code. Effort: **S**.
4. **End-of-day review's 5 questions (round 9) delivered via the same voice-log path** - let FilthE answer by voice
   at day's end instead of typing, using Spec 1's same parse-and-confirm pattern. Who: Builder. Effort: **S**.
5. **Weekly referral-ask reminder inside the coaching card**, sourced from the `weekly` report's job-completion data
   already planned. Who: Code. Effort: **S**.
6. **Address-matching safety net**: when a voice log's address doesn't match a known lead/door, flag it instead of
   guessing (part of Spec 1's step 5, called out separately because it's the main accuracy risk this round found
   unproven data on - budget real testing with FilthE's own voice on real Fremont-area addresses before trusting it).
   Who: Code + FilthE (test with real addresses). Effort: **S**.

## Watch out
- No source this round measured dictation accuracy specifically for street addresses or for Spanish speech - treat
  the confirm-before-save step as mandatory, not a nice-to-have, until FilthE's own real-world use proves it reliable.
- Don't build toward live mid-conversation AI coaching - it's not proven achievable at HMP's scale/budget even by
  companies that specialize in it, and anything that whispers instructions to FilthE mid-sale risks feeling like a
  script, working against the "salesman's right hand, not a robot talking for him" framing already set (O7).
- Coaching lines must never suggest talking a homeowner out of hesitation via anything touching the deductible or a
  promise about insurance paying - keep the rule-based lines to logistics/mindset/hail-evidence only.

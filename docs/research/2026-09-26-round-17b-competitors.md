## Biggest new find
**ABC Seamless of Fremont** is a same-town rival. Its franchise brand has complaints about warranties not being honored. HMP's honest pitch: "one local company, not a franchise; the same office answers you in year 5."

## Gaps HMP can win
1. None of the 15 showed a Spanish website, ads or reviews.
2. Local complaints keep repeating: slow jobs and warranties not honored. The claim tracker plus a "we call you back" promise answers both.
3. Nobody in this market showed CertainTeed SELECT ShingleMaster. That's unverified; check CertainTeed's contractor locator.
4. About half of them showed no financing offer. A GreenSky or Service Finance dealer account would stand out.
5. A GAF roof certification plus the James Hardie siding certification: no company above showed both.

## Certification to get first
**GAF Master Elite.** It's the most recognized badge in this market, and it unlocks the 50-year Golden Pledge warranty pitch.
- Requirements: state registration, insurance, financial checks, yearly GAF volume, GAF training, and requalifying every year.
- The fee and volume numbers weren't found; confirm them with a GAF rep.

Second choice: **James Hardie Elite Preferred**, since HMP already installs Hardie lap siding.
[CORRECTED 2026-09-27 (round 43 verify): James Hardie's real tiers are Enrolled -> Select -> Preferred
-> Elite - "Elite Preferred" isn't a real tier name, it conflates the top two. See round 33's update.]

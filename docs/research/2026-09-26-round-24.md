## Details

### Reviews and retention
- Google's policy (2026 update) explicitly bans: paying/discounting for reviews, review gating
  (asking only happy customers), review quotas for staff, and naming staff in solicited reviews.
  Breaking it risks stripped reviews or a suspended Google Business Profile. [1][2]
- FTC's review rule (effective Oct 21, 2024) makes incentivized/insider/gated reviews a federal
  violation with penalties up to ~$51,744 each; first enforcement warning letters went out Dec 2025. [3]
- What IS legal and works: ask everyone, ask fast (within 24 hrs, by text), give a direct link/QR
  code, keep the wording plain ("if you're happy, a quick review helps other homeowners find us").
  A QR code on the invoice/completion packet is a good passive reinforcement but not enough alone -
  the active text ask is what gets responses. [9]
- Completion packet contents contractors are advised to hand over: manufacturer warranty
  registration + confirmation, workmanship warranty in writing, before/after photos, material data
  sheets, paid/lien-waiver documentation - useful at resale, insurance claims, or a future warranty
  call. [4][5] Registration windows are often 30-60 days post-install; missing the window can void
  material coverage. [4]

### Door-to-door rules by city (FilthE already has permits for all five - this is hours/no-knock only)
- **Lincoln:** 8 AM-8 PM, any day (LMC ch. 5.36). Permit requires background check, expires within 1
  year, must be shown to police or a resident on request. Posted "No Soliciting" signs are enforced. [6]
- **Omaha:** 8 AM-6 PM per the city's peddler permit rules (nonprofits exempt from the permit
  itself, not from courtesy hours). [7]
- **Fremont:** Ordinance No. 3970 (Municipal Code ch. 10, sec. 10-201/10-202) - 30-day permit,
  carried on person, revocable for after-hours operation, high-pressure sales, false/fictitious
  affiliation claims, or ignoring a posted "No Peddlers/Solicitors" sign. Exact start/end clock times
  weren't in the pages found this round - **unverified**, confirm with Fremont PD (725 N Park) before
  the app states a specific hour. [8]
- **Columbus:** Permit applications taken 8 AM-4 PM Mon-Fri at Columbus PD (that's office hours to
  apply, not knock hours). The ordinance's actual hours-of-solicitation section (code ch. 110)
  wasn't reachable this round - **unverified**, needs a follow-up call to Columbus PD. [10]
- **Schuyler:** No peddler/solicitor chapter surfaced in search, and its code-library site wasn't
  reachable this round - **unverified**, needs a direct call to the Schuyler city clerk (1103 B St). [11]
- Nearby small-NE-town pattern for context only (not applied to any HMP target city): several small
  Nebraska towns' ordinances (e.g. Central City, Wood River) default to daytime-only solicitation
  (roughly 8/9 AM-5/9 PM) with an exception for a prior appointment - useful as a sanity check that
  Fremont/Columbus/Schuyler likely follow a similar shape, but not a substitute for their actual text.

## Watch out
- Never make a review ask conditional on anything, never filter who gets asked, never suggest
  wording that praises a specific employee by name in a way that reads like a quota - all against
  Google's 2026 policy and the FTC rule. [1][2][3]
- The city hours above marked "unverified" should not be hard-coded as fact in the app without a
  human confirming with the city - show them as "typical hours, confirm locally" until then.

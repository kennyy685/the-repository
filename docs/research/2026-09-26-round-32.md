# Research round 32 (backlog track)

Two backlog topics: selling the HMP App to other roofers, and NE bank/credit union home-improvement
loans as a no-dealer-fee option. Egress was blocked for most vendor/lender pages this round (nufcu.org,
catenergy.ne.gov, roofingcontractor.com all refused by the proxy) - findings below lean on WebSearch
summaries, flagged unverified where the primary page couldn't be opened.

## What to work on next (ranked)

1. **Don't price or pitch the HMP App to outside roofers yet - not ready to sell.** Effort: S. Who: FilthE/Boss (decision), Code (later).
   The app has one customer (HMP) and no multi-company data separation. The one real precedent found
   (Construct CRM, a Delray Beach vertical-SaaS builder now sold through building-supply distributors as
   white-label) took an established distributor relationship to reach contractors at scale - not a
   solo builder cold-pitching roofers. Selling before the product is proven on HMP's own jobs risks
   time spent on other companies' bugs instead of HMP's leads. Revisit once HMP has run real jobs
   through Leads -> Claims -> Done for a full season.
2. **When ready, price flat-tier not per-seat, and treat "getting more leads and signed jobs" as HMP's own real pilot.** Effort: M. Who: FilthE/Boss.
   2026 roofing-software pricing splits two ways: legacy per-seat tools (AccuLynx ~$100+/user,
   $250+/mo Essential; JobNimbus ~$225/mo) vs. newer flat-rate tools with no per-user fee (QuoteIQ
   $30-$700/mo tiers; FieldFuze $49-$799/mo flat tiers) [1][2]. A small crew-based shop like HMP (or a
   sibling small roofer) would balk at per-seat pricing that punishes adding a crew guy. Flat monthly
   tiers by feature level fit HMP's own situation and would fit a first outside customer the same way.
3. **Point homeowners who ask about financing at Nebraska's Dollar & Energy Saving Loan program first, not a dealer-fee lender.** Effort: S. Cost: free. Who: FilthE.
   Nebraska's state energy office runs a statewide loan program through local banks/credit unions at
   simple rates of 5%, 3.5% or less (some utility tie-ins as low as 1-3%), terms up to 15 years for
   home/building improvements [3]. This is a state program, not a dealer/reseller loan, so no dealer
   fee to disclose or manage - just a name to give homeowners. Unverified this round: whether siding/
   roofing specifically qualifies (search summaries didn't confirm the eligible-improvement list) and
   NUFCU's/BCU's specific unsecured home-improvement loan terms (pages blocked) - see backlog below.
4. **Keep a state-loan + credit-union name card ready as the "no dealer fee" alternative to third-party retail financing (Hearth/Wisetack, round 21/30).** Effort: S. Who: FilthE.
   University of Nebraska FCU and Builders Credit Union both advertise unsecured home-improvement
   loans with no application fee (per search summaries, page fetch blocked) [4][5]. Good middle option
   between "pay cash" and a dealer-fee retail lender for homeowners who don't want financing baked into
   the contract price.

## Quick tips for FilthE (no build needed)
- Don't quote or demo the app to another roofer yet - it's not there.
- If a homeowner asks about financing without a claim, mention the state Dollar & Energy Saving Loan
  program and a local credit union (NUFCU, BCU) as options with no dealer fee, alongside Hearth/Wisetack.
- Never say a loan program can replace or waive the deductible - it's a separate homeowner choice, not
  part of the storm-claim contract.

## Watch out
- Selling software to competitors before HMP proves it on its own jobs is a distraction from the
  bottleneck (knocking, claims), same lesson as round 1/2.
- Financing lines are informational only - HMP names options, doesn't broker, underwrite or take a
  cut; keep clear of anything that reads like arranging credit terms as part of the storm-damage sale
  (44-8601 to 44-8611 territory).
- Several of this round's sources are WebSearch summaries, not opened pages (proxy blocked nufcu.org,
  catenergy.ne.gov, dwee.nebraska.gov, roofingcontractor.com) - treat rates/fees as unconfirmed until
  someone reaches those pages directly or calls the lender.

1. **Don't chase GAF Master Elite for financing terms alone - the reduced-fee promo (if still live) applies to both Master Elite and plain Certified contractors, not just Master Elite.** Effort: S. Who: FilthE (decide later, not now).
   The one concrete precedent found is a 2012 GAF/Wells Fargo program giving reduced fees for low/no-interest

   lower bar than Certified. Search summaries couldn't confirm today's Service Finance Company dealer-fee

   without any GAF tier. HMP doesn't need Master Elite just for financing access - a direct dealer account
   with Service Finance or a similar consumer lender is worth checking on its own, tier or no tier.
2. **James Hardie's Select tier is the realistic near-term target, not Preferred/Elite - it's the entry level that unlocks the full manufacturer warranty on installs.** Effort: M. Who: FilthE/Boss (enrollment), Code (track once enrolled).
   Select is described as the entry tier that qualifies installs for the full Hardie product warranty and
   signals the installer is authorized, insured, and installing to spec [2][3]. Preferred and Elite sit above
   it and require higher install volume, a longer track record, and GuildQuality customer-satisfaction scores
   (Elite Preferred: $1M+ liability, BBB standing, 90%+ "Likely to Recommend" score, install audits) [2] - out
   of reach for HMP right now. Getting to Select (install-to-spec + basic Hardie training) is realistic given
   The Edge job is already installing Hardie lap siding; it gives a homeowner-facing badge and access to the
   Find-a-Contractor directory, which is free lead flow.

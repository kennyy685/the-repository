## Summary
1. **A solo rookie's ramp should be volume-gated, not day-1-full-route.** Vendor D2D training sources
   describe a gradual ramp (roughly 10%→50%→75%→full over several weeks) with week 1 as practice/pitch
   drilling only — HMP has no team to shadow, so Practice Door roleplays substitute. This matches
   `benchmarks.json`'s new-rep inspection rate (0.5-2%) being roughly half the experienced rate (2-3.5%):
   the plan should not expect a booked inspection before ~day 20-25.
2. **Daily roleplay throughout the whole ramp, not just week 1**, is a repeated vendor claim (cuts ramp
   time) — folded into every block of `rookie_plan.json`, referencing round 27's `practice_drills.json`
   ids (all 10 ids verified present in that file).
3. **ADHD pacing = short blocks + a daily streak + one line per day**, not a long checklist. Each
   `rookie_plan.json` entry has exactly one `daily_goal` line (EN/ES) and 2-3 drill_ids — short and
   skimmable, matching CLAUDE.md's standing instruction for FilthE.
4. **Landlords/small PMs have real, legal trade associations to join** (MOPOA - Omaha metro, REOMA -
   Lincoln, Omaha Landlords Association) — a business-line/membership-directory path, not cold texts or
   bought lists. Membership fee amounts are unverified this round.
5. **HOA boards are reachable legally and for free via the Nebraska Secretary of State's Corporate &
   Business Search** (HOAs are registered nonprofit corps; the search gives the registered agent/
   management company office line) — no personal-cell or resident-list scraping needed.
6. **HOA roofs get replaced on a budget cycle (reserve studies, every 3-5 years) and the claim path is
   fundamentally different from a single-family home**: master-policy claims belong to the association,
   never a unit owner, and any per-owner deductible cost is a special assessment — HMP must never imply
   coverage or deductible help to an individual unit owner in an HOA.
7. **Vendor packet (COI, W-9, lien-waiver template) is a real prerequisite** PMs/HOAs ask for before a
   bid is even considered — worth having ready as a PDF, not something FilthE improvises at the door.

## Useful details

### Rookie 60-day ramp (topic 1)
- Week 1 (days 1-8): learn the opener cold, practice tone/body language in short bursts, no real-door
  quota yet — HMP substitutes Practice Door roleplays for the shadowing a real D2D team would normally do.
- Days 9-16: start real knocking at low volume (10 doors/day) so the habit sticks without burnout; log
  every door.
- Days 17-24: build toward a full route (20-25 doors), always end with "one small ask" (round 27's rule).
- Days 25-32: full daily volume (25-35 doors), work toward the first booked inspection; show photos
  before paperwork (round 31's inspection-walk sequence).
- Days 33-40: lock in the legally required lines every time — 3-day cancel right, honest deductible
  answers, EN and ES.
- Days 41-48: full volume plus 3-5 follow-ups/week on older leads — **door revisits, or calls/texts only
  to homeowners who gave their number and asked to be followed up with** (tightened from the scout's
  draft to match CLAUDE.md's no-cold-text / no-bought-list rule exactly).
- Days 49-60: full independent days, weekly review of numbers against benchmarks, adjust what isn't
  working.
- Sourcing: repcard.com and knockbase.com (D2D onboarding structure), dialfyne.com (daily roleplay claim),
  hyperbound (ramp-volume curve), takecontroladhd.com (ADHD pacing — general ADHD-productivity source, not
  sales-specific, applied here as a synthesis), userevenueos.com (weekly review cadence). All vendor-blog
  sourced; the ADHD-pacing application to a sales ramp is this round's own synthesis, not a cited study.
- Watch out: vendor "80-120 doors/day" and "full quota by month 4-5" figures are large-team benchmarks,
  not appropriate for a solo rookie — not used in the plan.

### Landlords / small PMs / HOA boards (topic 2)
- **MOPOA** (Metro Omaha Property Owners Association): 500+ landlord members, 402-210-0273.
- **REOMA** (Real Estate Owners & Managers Association, Lincoln): 130+ members / 7,000+ units,
  reomalincoln.org.
- **Omaha Landlords Association**: 402-932-1022.
- Membership fee amounts for any of the three: not verified this round.
- **Nebraska Secretary of State Corporate & Business Search** (sos.nebraska.gov): free, gives an HOA's
  registered agent and principal office — the legal way to find the management company's office line
  instead of contacting individual residents.
- HOA sales angle: reserve studies happen every 3-5 years and often flag the roof; offering a free
  roof-condition inspection ahead of the board's next reserve-study review or annual budget vote gives
  them real numbers before that meeting — a legitimate value-add, not pressure.
- Vendor packet: certificate of insurance (COI), W-9, and a lien-waiver template are commonly required by
  PMs/HOAs before they'll consider a bid or release payment.
- Multi-property pricing: one inspection and one quote covering all of an owner's buildings; a portfolio
  discount (5-25% claimed by vendor sources) is unverified and a pricing decision for the boss, not
  assumed here.
- **HOA legal boundary (important):** the roof is common property under the association's master
  insurance policy — an individual unit owner cannot file that claim, and any per-owner deductible cost
  typically comes back as a special assessment, not something HMP can offer to cover or discuss. Never
  imply otherwise to a unit owner.
- Opening lines (business line only, EN/ES) — see the five scripts in the scout hand-off; all pass the
  "registered" not "licensed" check, no insurance-pays promise, no deductible talk, no claims-negotiation
  language.

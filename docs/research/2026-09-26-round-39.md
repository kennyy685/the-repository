## Details

### Insured Homeowners Protection Act (44-8601-8608) - full text now in docs/legal/
- **44-8601**: names the Act (44-8601 to 44-8608), nothing else.
- **44-8605 (post-loss assignment of benefits):** if a residential contractor takes an assignment of
  insurance rights/benefits from a homeowner after a loss:
  (a) may be named copayee on the claim payment;
  (b) must send the assignment to the insurer within 5 business days of signing;
  (c) must include a statement that the contractor made no assurance the loss will be fully covered,
  plus specific bold/14-point capitalized notice text (quoted in the statute) telling the homeowner
  what they're signing away;
  (d) can't impair a mortgagee's interest listed on the policy;
  (e) can't block the insurer from talking directly to the homeowner or mortgagee.
  Also directs the Dept. of Insurance to strictly enforce insurers' duty (44-1540(13)) to explain any
  claim denial or lowball offer in plain terms - a fact worth knowing when coaching FilthE for the
  adjuster meeting (a denial/lowball has to come with a real explanation, homeowner can push back).
- **44-8606 (itemized description):** before starting repair/replacement work, the contractor must
  give **both the insured and the insurer** an itemized description: work to be done, materials,
  labor, fees, and the total amount to be paid - but this doesn't stop the contractor from later
  adding other necessary work tied to the same covered loss.
- **44-8608 (void contract):** any violation of this whole Act voids the contract. High-stakes, cheap
  to comply with - this is a checklist item, not a redesign.
- Full text saved: `docs/legal/44-8601.txt`, `44-8605.txt`, `44-8606.txt`, `44-8608.txt` (44-8602,
  44-8603, 44-8604, 44-8607 were already saved from earlier rounds - the Act is now fully captured).

### Fremont permit + local registration (fremontne.gov, direct fetch)
- Roof shingle repair/replacement and siding repair/replacement both explicitly listed as requiring a
  building permit + inspection (homeowners doing their own work still need the permit, just not a
  license). Source page also lists what does NOT need a permit (painting, tiling, carpet, small
  free-standing decks) - useful context, not a roofing/siding exemption.
- Separate from the state Contractor Registration Act: City Council Resolution 2019-049 (2019-03-26)
  requires anyone doing for-hire construction work needing a building permit, inside Fremont city
  limits or its 2-mile zoning jurisdiction, to register with the City: $65 fee, completed application,
  $250,000 min liability insurance naming the City of Fremont as additional insured, and proof of
  registration with the Nebraska Dept. of Labor. Register at the Fremont Municipal Building, 3rd
  floor, 400 E Military Ave (Building Permits/Inspections office: 402-727-2636).
- Omaha (search-summary only, city sites blocked curl): commonly cited that roof repairs/replacements
  over 2 squares (200 sq ft) need a permit; 2018 IRC/IBC in effect; permits issued at the Omaha Civic
  Center, 1819 Farnam St, Room 1110. Not opened directly - treat as unverified until re-checked.
- Lincoln (search-summary only): has a dedicated "Re-Roof Policy" page under Building & Safety;
  content not opened (site blocked). Not verified this round.

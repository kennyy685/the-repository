## Details

### Homeowner-screen order (ties to option-b.html)
- Reps who open with photos and a plain explanation close more than reps who open with a tablet full of
  pricing - "solving the wrong problem first." Photos: flip between wide shots and close-ups (6-12), not
  just one shot. Matches HailHunter's existing s1 (photos) step.
- Presenting in person and closing at the appointment (not emailing after) closes at 2-3x the rate.
  Option B is built as an in-person screen already - keep it that way, never turn it into a leave-it-and-
  email-later PDF.
- Proposal step should show a timeline (when work starts, how long it takes) alongside the pricing tiers -
  s2 has the Good/Better/Best tiers; adding one line with a rough start/duration window would close a gap.
- A damage map (not just photos) is named as its own step in more than one source - not yet in option-b.

### Referral engine, week-by-week (first 10 jobs)
- **Day of job completion**: yard sign goes up with the homeowner's permission (already free, already a
  habit worth locking in every job, not just some).
- **Within 24 hours of completion**: ask for a Google review by text/call while the work is fresh - this
  is the single highest-leverage ask in the research (most homeowners read reviews before they call).
  Target: 10 reviews in the first 90 days.
- **Same week**: hand-deliver door hangers to the 6-10 closest neighbors at the job site (not a cold
  street - a house that just saw a crew working next door). This is the "neighbor note" HailHunter already
  has as an optional print piece (CLAUDE.md: don't push it as the main plan, but it's a good fit right
  after a completed job specifically).
- **Within a week or two of completion**: handwritten thank-you note (D17's already-decided answer - no
  gift cards, no cash). This stays the only referral "incentive."
- Repeat every job, not just the first 10 - the first 10 just establish the habit before volume makes it
  easy to skip.

## Watch out
- A referral-rock source pitches paying homeowners cash/gift-card referral fees (~$500, framed as cheaper
  than paid ads) - **do not do this**. FilthE already decided (D17) handwritten notes only; paying for
  referrals isn't itself a deductible-waiver violation, but it's the kind of "we'll pay you" language that
  reads badly next to 44-8604 in an insurance-claim conversation, and it's simply not FilthE's decided plan.
  Don't reintroduce it.
- Door hangers convert at only 1-2% cold - fine as free awareness after a job (warm, not cold), don't treat
  them as a standalone lead engine.
- Everything above is search-summary sourced (WebFetch/curl blocked on spotio.com, referralrock.com, and
  every other vendor/blog tried) - treat specifics (2-3x close rate, 10 reviews/90 days, $500 referral fee)
  as industry-general claims, not verified numbers, until a primary source opens up.

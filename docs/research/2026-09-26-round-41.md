   `load_acs`** - Code, effort S. Same file, same pattern as `med_value`/`med_year` already there (one more
   entry in `ACS_TABLES`, one more `hud.py` field). Why it helps: a rough income floor helps `everyday.py`
   (non-storm old-house door lists) and `doorscore.py` avoid ranking low-income blocks with old homes above

## Watch out
- Don't confuse "this sandboxed session can't reach a domain" with "the engine can't." Re-test from wherever
  `hh.py refresh` actually executes before writing anything off as permanently blocked, and before spending
  effort on a workaround for a limit that may not exist there.
- Income data is a proxy, not a fact - never surface a specific household's income anywhere customer-facing;
  it's a block-group average, for door-list ranking only, same handling as existing ACS fields.

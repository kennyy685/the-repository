# Research round 42 (2026-09-26): Competitor teardown — where 12 field-sales, roofing-CRM, hail-data and
photo/estimate apps fall short, and how the HMP App beats it

**FilthE asked for this because he thinks past rounds haven't dug hard enough.** This one digs: the egress
proxy opened up mid-round (FilthE's doing), so instead of only search-result snippets, most of the complaints
below are pulled **directly from the apps' own Google Play Store pages** — real 1-3★ reviews, fetched and
parsed by this session, not summarized by someone else. Every app but Roofr (browser-only, no phone app) had
an Android app; all ten of those were fetched and are marked **[Play-verified]** with the real star rating
and review count. G2/Capterra/Reddit content stayed snippet-only — Capterra blocks bots itself, reddit.com's
`.json` API blocked us with its own anti-bot wall (not the proxy), and itunes.apple.com / old.reddit.com /
salesrabbit.com / hailtrace.com / acculynx.com were refused by org policy or the site's own Cloudflare — those
are marked **[review-snippet]**. Three vendor pricing pages (spotio.com, companycam.com, roofr.com) were
fetched directly and are marked **[vendor-verified]**.

**Bottom line:** every one of the 12 has the same three diseases — buggy mobile apps (10/12, confirmed by
real reviews), pricing that hides behind a demo call or balloons with per-seat add-ons (6/12, confirmed), and
being only one slice of the job (finding storms, OR canvassing, OR photos, OR claims — never all of it). Zero
of the 12 do real Spanish, zero have built-in sales-roleplay coaching, zero do hands-free voice logging at the
door. Those three gaps are exactly what the HMP App's Right Hand, Practice Door and planned voice log already
aim at — this round's job is proving that out loud and closing the two real gaps HMP still has (photos, and
digital signing).

## What to work on next (ranked)
1. **Say "one flat price, no seat math, no setup fee" everywhere the app is shown (later, when sold).** The
   single most common non-technical complaint across these 12 is billing: SalesRabbit's real cost balloons to
   $100-150+/user/month once add-ons stack, JobNimbus lands $300-1,000+/month, Leap took **$555 from a user's
   bank account "for no reason"** and needs 45 days' notice to cancel, SPOTIO's own pricing page (fetched
   directly) shows **no dollar figures at all** — you must sit through a sales call. HMP's edge here is real
   and cheap to state. — **FilthE/Boss, S**
2. **Get FilthE actually using Practice Door before his first real door.** Round 2 already found he hasn't
   knocked yet. AI-roleplay sales training is a whole separate paid product category for everyone else
   (PitchMonster, Roofing Sales Academy, OnCue) — none of the 12 competitors here build it into their
   CRM/canvassing tool. HMP already has it. Use it. — **FilthE, S**
3. **Finish the Spanish work already on the roadmap (T73/T80/T81).** Checked all 12: only Jobber and AccuLynx
   even mention Spanish, and both are a bolt-on translation layer ("phrasing gets awkward in less common
   screens" — Jobber). None read the legal notices aloud in Spanish or carry a fixed bilingual glossary. This
   is a real, defensible difference once it's actually finished and used at a door. — **Code, M**
4. **Stress-test the app on bad signal / low battery before FilthE trusts it at a rural door.** The #1
   technical complaint, almost app-for-app: crashes, endless re-login loops, "won't sync," lost notes/photos
   mid-upload (SalesRabbit, SPOTIO, CompanyCam, JobNimbus, Jobber, Leap, HailTrace, Hail Recon all show this
   in their own 1-star reviews). HMP hasn't been tested under real field conditions yet either — don't inherit
   this failure mode. — **Code/QA (qa-tester), M**
5. **Finish voice logging (T79/T82).** No competitor here has cracked hands-free door logging — the closest
   analogues (Voze, Conquer Voice Plus, RepBud) are generic sales tools, not roofing-specific, and cost extra.
   First win: confirm the iPhone mic actually works (Mac testing already flagged it as blocked). — **Code, M**
6. **Build the one-tool story as the sale pitch for later (Now → Knock → + → Leads → Money).** Every
   competitor here is one slice: HailTrace/Hail Recon only find storms, Knockbase/RepCard/SPOTIO/SalesRabbit
   only canvass, CompanyCam only takes photos, Roofr only measures/quotes, AccuLynx/JobNimbus/Leap/Jobber only
   run the office. CompanyCam and Roofr even publish an official integration page because neither is enough
   alone. A future customer of the HMP App is buying out of paying for 2-4 of these. — **FilthE/Builder, S**
7. **Decide (don't subscribe) on a one-off certified hail report for disputed claims only.** HailTrace's real
   edge is meteorologist-certified reports (15+ meteorologists, 4 tiers) that carry weight with an adjuster on
   a contested claim — worth roughly $230 for one map [review-snippet, price not vendor-verified], not a
   monthly plan. Use it if/when a claim gets pushed back, not before. — **Boss decision, then FilthE, S**
8. **Build the lead-photo gap.** CompanyCam's whole business is unlimited GPS/time-stamped photos organized
   per job — and CLAUDE.md already flags "photos per lead not yet" as a known gap in the HMP App's lead
   tracker. This is the one place a real competitor is doing something HMP genuinely hasn't built. — **Builder,
   M**
9. **Look at a pay-per-report measurement option, don't build one.** Roofr's $13-19/report satellite/aerial
   measurement [vendor-verified pricing] and AccuLynx's EagleView integration are both real, useful, and
   expensive to replicate. For occasional tricky roofs, paying per report beats building an in-house measuring
   tool HMP doesn't need daily. — **Boss decision, M to use, don't build**
10. **Explicitly decide NOT to chase gamification/leaderboards.** SalesRabbit and SPOTIO lean hard on
    team leaderboards and rep competition — irrelevant with FilthE as a one-to-two-person sales operation
    today. Naming this as "not now" keeps the team from wasting effort copying a feature aimed at 10+ rep
    teams. — **FilthE, S (a decision, not a build)**

## 1. Per-app teardown

### SalesRabbit — field sales / canvassing
**Price:** Lite free (1 user); Team $59/user/mo; Pro $75/mo list ($49/mo annual); add-ons (DataGrid AI
$19-31/user/mo, Digital Contracts $13-20/user/mo, weather maps $19-31/user/mo) push real cost to
**$100-150+/user/month**; one-time setup fee **$399-$4,999** [review-snippet, multiple pricing-aggregator
sites agree].
**Reps love:** AI-assisted lead prioritization and territory mapping; works well for small D2D teams;
smooth/easy initial onboarding per several reviewers [review-snippet].
**Top complaints** (Google Play, **3.01★ / 689 reviews** [Play-verified, fetched 2026-09-26]):
1. "Despite having paid them thousands, it takes months of back and forth to resolve bugs (if you are
   lucky). App isn't maintained anymore." [Play-verified]
2. "The December update has really ruined it. Won't load areas, won't sync, won't allow re-syncing and
   customer service was not helpful." [Play-verified]
3. "You cannot export your data without paying them money — and they are not upfront about that." [Play-verified]
4. "$399 setup fee" just to bulk-upload 15-20 addresses, discovered only after an hour of trying. [Play-verified]
5. "They deleted my bad review after responding... I pay $60 for a worse platform." [Play-verified]
6. Pin placement lands on the wrong lot in new construction/rural areas — a years-old, unresolved complaint
   [review-snippet, G2/Capterra].
7. Lead and note data loss reported across multiple time periods — looks systemic, not one-off
   [review-snippet, G2].
**Missing:** no Spanish, no sales-roleplay coaching, no built-in claims/adjuster workflow, one-way Salesforce
sync only.

## 4. What they do better than us today (blunt)

- **AccuLynx: real aerial roof measurement (EagleView) and a decade-deep insurance-restoration workflow.**
  HMP's claims tracker is younger and unproven in the field. Fix: keep building the supplement/depreciation
  chase list already underway (round 9) — don't try to match AccuLynx's full depth, just the flow FilthE
  actually needs.
- **Roofr and AccuLynx: automated satellite/drone roof measurement in minutes.** HMP has zero measurement
  automation today. Fix: don't build this — budget-decide a pay-per-report tool (Roofr, $13-19/report, no
  subscription) for the roofs where a fast, accurate number actually matters.
- **Leap SalesPro and most CRMs: e-signature/digital contract signing at the door.** HMP's contract is still a
  printed draft with a blank registration-number line (T64) and a physical 3-day cancel form; competitors
  close paperwork in minutes on a tablet. Fix: real gap, but wait for the deferred lawyer review to clear the
  current contract language before building digital signing — the deductible and cancel-notice wording is
  legally sensitive (44-8607, 69-1601).
- **SalesRabbit/SPOTIO: gamification and multi-rep territory management at scale.** Not relevant with FilthE
  selling alone today — explicitly deprioritized (win move #10) — but becomes a real gap if HMP adds more
  sales reps later.
- **CompanyCam: unlimited photo storage with slick before/after sharing.** This is CompanyCam's entire

## Watch out
- Every "vendor claim" price above (JobNimbus, AccuLynx, Leap base rate, Hail Recon, HailTrace) came from
  third-party pricing blogs, not the vendor's own page — those sites monetize by steering readers to
  alternatives, so treat the numbers as directional, not exact.
- Play Store review counts (e.g. "689 reviews" for SalesRabbit) are the headline total shown on the page;
  Google doesn't expose a full star-by-star breakdown in the page source, so "X★ average" is solid but the
  1/2/3-star split is not.
- Nothing here touches Nebraska law: all of it is product/UX/pricing complaints about other companies'
  software, not legal claims. No idea in this round involves a deductible, a claim promise, or negotiating on
  a homeowner's behalf.

# Research round 6 (2026-09-26): more sub work + realistic first-knock numbers

**Bottom line:**
1. **Sub work comes fastest with a ready sub packet and a call within 48-72 hours of a storm.** Shamrock Roofing
   (Omaha) publicly recruits sub crews: call them first.
2. **Rookie numbers are small, and that's normal.** Expect about 10-15 doors an hour and 20-40% of doors answering.
   About 2-4% of doors become inspections once you're good (near 1% at first), and 30-60% of inspections sign. Judge
   week 1 by doors knocked and follow-ups, not by sales.

Caveat: the scouts' page-opening tool was blocked, so they worked from search summaries only. The conversion numbers come
from sales-software blogs: treat them as typical ranges, not studies.

## Rookie benchmarks (industry-typical, unverified)
- 10-15 doors an hour in normal neighborhoods; 25-35 on a tight route.
- 20-40% of doors answer. Evenings 5-8 PM are best.
- Doors to booked inspections: under 1% for rookies, 2-3% for competent reps, 1 in 25 for top storm crews.
- Inspections to signed: 30-60% (insurance/storm jobs close higher than cash jobs).
- Reps who last: log every door, follow up the same day (most sales take 5+ touches, and most reps quit after 2), and knock at the same times every week.
- Winter (Dec-Feb) is the slowest season. Short days cut both pace and answers.

**Legal:** Fremont requires a Sales & Solicitation permit (Ordinance 3970, sec. 10-202). It's valid for **30 days**, so renew it.
Carry it with you and respect "No Solicitors" signs. Nearby counties cap knocking at 9 AM-8 PM; confirm Fremont's hours
(fremontne.gov DocumentCenter/View/840).

## Watch out
- Even as a sub: no deductible talk (44-8604), no negotiating claims.
- Skip pay-to-join "sub networks" with no named company behind them.

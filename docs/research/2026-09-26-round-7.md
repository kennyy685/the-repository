## Bottom line
1. **Prices:** roofs are right. **Hardie is priced low** (Omaha is about $1,000-1,500 a square vs our $900-1,200). Vinyl may run
   slightly high (weak evidence). Add ranges for the extras homeowners ask about.
2. **After the estimate:** Good/Better/Best proposal → sign on the phone ("sign to confirm"; the paper contract stays the legal
   one until the lawyer's OK) → contract auto-filled → material order list → completion certificate + review request.
3. **Material order math** is standard and easy to code. **Ice & water shield on every eave is Nebraska code**, not an upsell.
4. **Coaching per step:** get both decision makers there, stop pitching at buying signals, send 2 reminder texts, be on the roof
   with the adjuster in the same hour, and explain the 3-day cancel out loud.

## 2. After the estimate (ranked)
1. **Good/Better/Best proposal + finger signature** (M). Label it "sign to confirm"; the printed contract is the legal document.
2. **Contract auto-filled from the lead** (S): the existing draft with the 3-day cancel + deductible notice, never handwritten.
   **The 3-day right may not apply when the homeowner called HMP first**, so the app should ask "Who reached out first?" (verify
   against the statute).
3. **Material order list** to text to the supplier (S-M).
4. **Completion checklist → completion certificate → review request** (S).
5. Crew scheduling grid (M): later, when there are more crews.

## 3. Material order math (trade rules of thumb)
- **Roof:** 3 bundles/square; waste 10% gable, 12-15% hip, 15-20% cut-up. Starter 1 bundle/100 ft of eave + rake. Ridge cap
  (ridge + hip)/33 ft per bundle. Drip edge (eave + rake) x 1.10 in 10-ft sticks. Synthetic underlayment 10 sq/roll. **Ice &
  water from the eave to 24" inside the wall, every eave (IRC R905.1.2, Nebraska cold zone).** Nails: default to 6/shingle
  (wind), about 480/sq. Pipe boots counted, not calculated. Ridge vent = ridge ft / carton coverage (confirm with the supplier).
- **Vinyl:** net wall area/100 + 10-15% waste (15-20% gables). J-channel = the perimeter of every opening + rakes. One corner
  post per corner, sticks = ceil(wall height / post length). Starter = bottom-course length. House wrap = area x 1.05 per roll.
  Nails: ask the supplier.
- **Hardie 8.25" at 7" exposure:** about 15 pieces/square + 10-15% waste. Hot-dip galvanized nails. Trim by linear foot. Caulk
  and paint: ask the supplier (no reliable formula).
- **Gutters:** eave feet; 1 downspout per 30-40 ft (at least 1 per run); hangers every 18" in snow country; end caps 2 per run;
  miters 1 per corner.
- **Best data:** one photo of a real HMP supplier invoice gives real Fremont unit prices.

## 4. Coaching per step (for the Next-step cards)
- **Door → inspection set:** ask "Who else should be part of this decision? Will they be here?" It's a scheduling question,
  never a condition.
- **Inspection set:** 2 reminders (right away + 1-2 days before); call next-day bookings.
- **At the table:** questions about install, cancel or warranty are **buying signals**: answer, then ask for the decision; don't
  keep pitching.
- **Paperwork:** explain the 3-day cancel **out loud**, hand over both copies, and the buyer initials it.
- **Claim:** **be on the roof with the adjuster, the same hour**; document only, never negotiate.
- Steps 6b-8 (measure/order, build, get paid + review) need one more pass once page access works.

# Research round 8 (2026-09-26): learning from the best apps

FilthE's bar: not "it works" but "reliably improves the salesman and the business". The scouts studied Leap SalesPro, Roofr,
RoofLink, AccuLynx, JobNimbus, Hover, iRoofing (in-home selling); SalesRabbit, Spotio, RepCard, Knockbase (canvassing);
CompanyCam, Hover, EagleView, HailTrace and color visualizers (homeowner-facing reports); plus sales-training research (practice,
coaching). Page opening was blocked, so everything comes from search summaries. **Vendor lift numbers are marketing, not proof.**

## Free wins this week (no build)
- Show homeowners **Hardie Designer** (jameshardie.com/hardie-designer) or **Renoworks** to try siding colors on their own house.
- Before each knock block, pick **one thing to practice** that day.
- Say the reason at the door ("I saw hail marks on your gutters").

## Don't (yet)
- Paid tools: Hover scans, GAF QuickMeasure, HailTrace, Gong or Second Nature licenses. Practice Door and our engine already
  cover the core.
- Recording homeowners at the door. It's legal (Nebraska is one-party consent) but bad for trust.
- Leaderboards (one seller). Auto-texting anyone who didn't ask (TCPA).
- Financing "as low as $X/mo" needs a lender partner: a money decision for FilthE and the boss. Never next to deductible talk.

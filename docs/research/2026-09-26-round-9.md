# Research round 9 (2026-09-26): the first 90 days

Search summaries only (page opening blocked); the quit/response numbers are vendor-blog figures, so treat them as typical, not proven.

**Bottom line:** roughly 35-43% of new reps quit within 90 days, and the top named cause is unclear expectations, not hard
work. Reps who go 0-for-40 in week 1 and read it as failure rarely reach week 8. Judge the early weeks by activity and
logging, not sales.

## Week-by-week coaching plan
| Weeks | Daily routine | Watch | Common failure → fix | What the app says |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Knock + log every door; debrief after each block | Doors, doors logged, answer rate (20-40% normal) | A long streak of "no" feels like failing → celebrate logging, not yeses | "You knocked and logged every door today. That's the win." |
| 3-6 | Book every Interested into an inspection within 24-48 h | Inspections booked/week, same-day follow-up rate, inspection → signed | Interested leads go cold (most sales need 5+ touches; most reps stop after 1-2) → reminders at 48 h / 5 days / 10 days | "Which Interested doors haven't you touched in 3 days?" |
| 7-12 | Weekly look at the weakest step; ask for referrals within 48 h of a finished job | Referrals asked per job, close rate, neighbor jobs | Never asking, or asking too late → prompt at job completion | "Most of our work comes from people like you telling a friend or neighbor. If anyone comes to mind, we'd love an introduction." |

## End-of-day review (5 questions, our synthesis)
1. How many doors did I knock and log? 2. Which Interested leads did I follow up with? 3. One thing that worked?
4. One thing I'd change tomorrow? 5. Tomorrow's follow-ups and first knock?

## What the app should add (ranked)
1. **End-of-day review** after "Done for today": 5 quick questions, saved per day; answers feed Practice Door's field objections (S).
2. **Follow-up reminders** on every Interested at 48 h / 5 days / 10 days, shown in "Your next moves" (S).
3. **Phase-aware coaching:** week of tenure (1-2 / 3-6 / 7-12) changes the numbers shown and the nudge line (M).
4. **Referral ask** at job completion: a read-aloud script EN/ES, no discounts or payments (S).
5. **Rookie framing:** a logging-complete badge; "no answers" called normal (S).

Rules: referrals are compliments and introductions, never paid or discounted; nothing that touches a deductible (44-8604).

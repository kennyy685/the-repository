## Bottom line
A GBP listing is the single highest-payoff-for-effort thing on the whole board right now: it's
**free**, takes FilthE **about 30 minutes** to set up, and it's the thing homeowners actually see
when they search "roofer near me" or look HMP up after a knock - which nothing else HMP has built
does. Three things matter most:
1. **Set it up as a Service Area Business (SAB) with the address hidden.** HMP's mailing address
   (2600 Laverna St, Apt 50, Fremont) is a home apartment, not a place customers visit - Google's own
   rule for this is to enter the address for verification, then turn off "Show business address to
   customers" and list service-area towns instead. Skipping this is one of the most common reasons
   Google suspends a profile [2][3].
2. **Verification will very likely be video, not postcard**, in 2026 - film the phone/laptop showing
   the business name, then pan to a truck, signage, tools, or a job in progress. Postcard-by-mail is
   getting rarer, especially for SABs with no public storefront [1].
3. **Reviews are covered by the FTC's 2024 rule** (effective Oct 21, 2024): ask everyone the same way,
   never gate (routing happy customers to Google and unhappy ones to a private form is now a rule
   violation), never pay/discount/incentivize for a review, star rating, or sentiment. HMP's plan
   ("ask everyone, no incentives, no gating") is already lined up with this rule and with the
   deductible-rebate line in 44-8604 (never mention insurance-pay or deductible discounts in a review
   ask) [7][8].

## Click-by-click setup guide (FilthE, ~30 minutes)

1. Go to **google.com/business** (or search "Google Business Profile") and sign in with the Google
   account HMP will keep long-term - **decide this first**: FilthE's own Gmail, or a new
   `hmpsidingroofing@gmail.com`-style account so the boss can also be added as a manager later (this
   is D20 below - ask FilthE which he wants before creating it).
2. Click **"Manage now"** / **"Add your business to Google"**. Enter the business name exactly:
   **HMP Siding & Roofing LLC**.
3. Pick the **primary category: "Roofing contractor."** Then add secondary categories: **"Siding
   contractor"** and **"Gutter cleaning service"** (the closest official category to gutter work -
   Google's category list has no exact "gutter installation" category; list installation/gutters as
   line items under Services instead) [9][10].
4. When asked **"Do you want to add a location customers can visit?"** answer **No** - this makes it
   a Service Area Business. (If Google still asks for an address for verification, enter the Laverna
   St apartment - that's normal and it stays private; see step 7.)
5. **Service areas** - Google allows up to 20; pick by city/town, not zip codes, so it's easy to keep
   current: **Fremont, Omaha, Lincoln, Columbus, Schuyler, Blair, Wahoo, North Bend** (all inside
   Google's own "about a 2-hour drive" guidance from Fremont) [4][5]. Skip Lexington for GBP even
   though it's in the engine's `everyday_towns` list - it's a 2.5+ hour drive, past what Google
   treats as relevant, and could weaken how strongly HMP ranks in the closer towns that matter more.
6. Add the **business phone (402-889-3385)**, website (the HMP App link, or skip if none is public
   yet), and hours (or "by appointment" if that fits better).
7. **Verify.** Google will offer whichever method it decides fits (usually **video** for a new SAB in
   2026): film a short video of the phone/computer showing the business name on screen, then pan to a
   truck, a job site, tools, or signage that shows this is a real, active roofing business [1][2].
   Postcard-by-mail may be offered instead - if so, it goes to the Laverna St address on file
   (private, not shown publicly) and takes 5-14 days.
8. Once verified, **turn off the address**: Business Profile → Edit profile → Location → turn off
   "Show business address to customers" (some accounts show this as a toggle right after the
   service-area step; if not, it's under Business Information → Location) [2][3].
9. Write the **description and services** (bilingual - draft text below) and upload the **logo**
   (`docs/brand/app-icon.svg` or the full HMP logo) plus **10-20 starting photos**: the truck, a
   finished job, the team, before/after from a recent job (same shots as
   `data/photo_checklist.json`).
10. Turn on **messaging** if FilthE wants leads to be able to text the profile directly, and check the
    **Q&A** and **Reviews** tabs weekly - Google surfaces both publicly and anyone can post to them.

## Watch out
- **Never mention "we cover/waive/rebate your deductible" or "insurance will pay for this" anywhere
  on the profile, in posts, or in review responses** - that's the 44-8604 line CLAUDE.md already
  sets, and GBP is public and permanent (unlike a door conversation).
- **Never gate or incentivize reviews** - no "leave a good review for $X off," no routing only happy
  customers to Google. The FTC's 2024 rule (effective Oct 21, 2024) makes both a federal violation,
  not just a Google policy issue, with penalties in the tens of thousands per violation [7][8].
- **Say "registered," never "licensed"** in the description/services text, consistent with CLAUDE.md.
- **Egress was blocked on nearly every source domain this session** (support.google.com,
  roofr.com, servicetitan.com, jobnimbus.com, sterlingsky.ca, localfalcon.com, gmbeverywhere.com,
  ftc.gov, and more all returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED` on WebFetch). Every number and step above is
  WebSearch-summary sourced, cross-checked across at least 2-3 independent sites per claim, but not
  confirmed by opening Google's or the FTC's own page directly this session - flagged as
  medium-confidence, not primary-verified. A future round with different egress access should open
  `support.google.com/business/answer/9157481` and `ftc.gov`'s rule page directly to confirm word for
  word.
- The "Gutter cleaning service" vs. "gutter installation" category naming was inconsistent across
  sources - confirmed only that "Gutter cleaning service" exists as an official category name;
  whether a separate "gutter installation" category exists is unverified. Low stakes either way since
  the Services list under Roofing/Siding contractor can spell out installation regardless.
- Geotagging photos: sources disagreed on whether Google still reads EXIF geodata from uploaded
  photos (one source says Google strips it, another recommends geotagging anyway). Low cost either
  way - no need to resolve before acting.

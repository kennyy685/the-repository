# Round 54: the salesman's lens (what he already knows vs. what the app must do)

King, 2026-09-27. FilthE: "What would a salesman already know? Put yourself in multiple scenarios of being a salesman
for multiple types of business. How could our app truly help? ... Put yourself in a perspective where you can realize
what I mean." Thinking round (perspective-taking), no new web sources; it builds on rounds 1-53.

## The realization
A salesman already knows how to sell: how to talk, read people, handle "not interested", build trust. An app that
teaches that is noise to a pro, and it's the first thing a paying roofer (Aldaba) would turn off. What no salesman
has, however good he is:
1. **Eyes he doesn't have:** which of 2,000 roofs really got hit, who owns vs. rents, who is likely insured, who's home
   at 5 PM, which streets a rival already signed.
2. **A perfect memory:** 60 conversations a week; the lady on Elm who said "after my husband's surgery".
3. **A back office in his pocket:** price, contract, the 3-day cancel notice in EN + ES, the claim steps, the
   itemized list for the insurer.
4. **A partner who follows up:** most yeses die from silence, not from "no".
5. **Proof in hand:** hail size and date for this address, photos, "registered", reviews.
6. **Legal armor:** under pressure, one wrong sentence ("we'll take care of your deductible") ends the business.
Rule: **the app does 1-6 and stays out of the way of the selling.** At the door the phone stays in the pocket:
10 seconds before the knock, 5 seconds after, and one shared screen at the kitchen table.

## Scenarios (first person)
- **Veteran storm roofer, day 5 after the Columbus hail.** "I know how to talk. I don't know which roofs got hit
  hardest, who's the owner, who's home now, or where the out-of-state guys already signed." → Hot zones + ranked
  walk (have it). Missing: a one-tap "rival sign here" so saturated streets cool down on the map.
- **Same roofer, 3 weeks later.** "60 conversations in my head. I forgot the callback I promised." → Memory: as he
  walks up, the next-door card whispers the last visit ("Maria, husband's surgery, said come back Sat 10 AM"). After
  the door: hold-to-talk voice note, turned into a follow-up (T82).
- **Cash siding rep, 1950s houses.** "They always say 'we need to think about it'. I know that objection." What he
  lacks: the monthly payment on the spot (financing, T109/T110), a picture of THEIR house in the new color, and a
  follow-up that isn't pushy. → Monthly price on the quote, a before/after visual of the house, a polite nurture
  schedule.
- **Commercial rep with property managers.** "I know relationships. I can't know the minute hail hits one of my 40
  buildings." → Storm alert on your own accounts: "1.25 in hail hit Maple Ridge last night: call the manager, here's
  the hail report." Bid package + vendor packet (COI/W-9) ready to send.
- **Insurance-savvy rep.** "The money is in the policy. An ACV roof or a cosmetic exclusion means I'm wasting a week."
  → Photo of the declarations page, read by AI for OUR qualification only (never coverage advice to the homeowner):
  deductible, ACV vs. RCV, cosmetic exclusion. Turns T70 from a habit into a 10-second check.
- **Bilingual door (HMP's edge).** "I can sell in Spanish; the paperwork has to be Spanish too, by law." → Every
  notice, script and homeowner screen EN/ES (have it). Team handoff: a Spanish-first homeowner goes to Alex with the
  notes (a setter/closer handoff, like solar teams).
- **Other industries, one lesson each:** solar = setter/closer handoff and one qualifying document (their power bill;
  ours = the dec page). Pest/alarm summer reps = speed: one tap per door, next door with zero thinking (have it). Real
  estate = farming: be the roof guy of a neighborhood for years (anniversary touches, T154/T162). Car sales = the
  "T.O.": when stuck, bring in backup (call Alex or the boss from the lead).
- **The rookie (FilthE today).** Needs the Sale Guide, the scripts and Practice Door, but the coaching must fade as
  his numbers prove he has it: **Coach mode vs. Pro mode.** Pro mode is what a paying roofer wants on day 1.
- **The boss.** "Is it working?" without calling: doors, inspections, claims, money in Spanish (HMP HQ, boss view).

## Test for every feature
Does it (a) put him in front of more of the right people, (b) make each talk likelier to become a yes, (c) keep a
yes from dying afterward, or (d) keep him legal? And would a 10-year pro say "I can't get that anywhere else"?
If it only teaches what a pro already knows, it goes behind Coach mode or gets cut.

## Decided (FilthE, 2026-09-27)
"The teach-how-to-sell part is something different that you'll teach me outside the app." So there is no Coach
mode inside the app: the app is Pro only. Teaching lives in Practice Door and in lessons with Claude. What stays in
the app is what's legally required (69-1602 opener, cancel notice, 44-8606), what to collect, and what's next (T195).

## What this means for the app (ranked)
1. **Memory whisper + voice note:** last-visit note and best time on the next-door card; hold-to-talk after the door
   (T82, blocked on the iPhone voice test T79).
2. **Tool only (T195):** coaching text and how-to-sell lines leave the app; teaching moves to Practice Door + lessons.
3. **Storm alert on your accounts:** new hail over any lead, past customer or commercial contact → today's call list
   with the hail report attached.
4. **Dec page check:** photo → AI flags deductible / ACV / cosmetic exclusion, internal only.
5. **Rival-sign tap:** one tap marks a competitor's yard sign; the engine cools that street.
6. **Their house, new look:** a before/after visual for cash siding/roof sales (color and style picker).
7. **Team handoff:** send a lead to Alex (Spanish) or the boss, notes included.
Already strong, keep: hot zones + walk, one-tap doors, hail proof, homeowner view, quick price, follow-ups, legal steps.

## How to do it (about 10 minutes)

- Call **Monday-Friday, daytime**. Lincoln is open 8 AM-4 PM (verified). For Omaha, call during normal
  weekday hours.
- Each question below lists what we *think* the answer is, so you can just ask "is it still X?"
- After each call, **just tell Claude Code (or the AI hub chat) what they said, in plain words**, for example
  "Omaha: siding also needs a permit at 200 sq ft, license $100, talked to Sue." Code fills in the table
  below. You don't type into it.
- Get the first name of the person you talked to. If they mention a web page or PDF, ask them to email it and
  forward it to Code. Omaha's site is blocked for us, so an emailed copy counts as the written source.

**Opening line:** "Hi, this is Kenny Cruz with HMP Siding & Roofing in Fremont. We do roofing and siding on
houses. I have three quick questions about re-roof and re-side permits in [city]."

### Call 1: Omaha Permits & Inspections, 402-444-5233

If nobody answers, try the main line 402-444-5350 or email permit.info@cityofomaha.org.

1. "For **siding** replacement on a house, when do we need a permit? Is it the same **200 square feet
   (2 squares)** as a roof, or is a permit needed for any siding job?"
2. "Roofing and siding contractors need a **Class E** license, right? Is it **$100** to start plus an
   **$8 tech fee**?"
3. "Is the insurance minimum **$300,000** liability, and is the bond **$10,000**?"
- Bonus if there's time: "What does a re-roof permit cost, and how long does it usually take?"

### Call 2: Lincoln Building & Safety, 402-441-7521

Open Mon-Fri 8 AM-4 PM. Email: plandev@lincoln.ne.gov.

1. "When a re-roof or re-side **does** need a permit, what's the **fee**? And does replacing **siding**
   need a permit at all?"
   (Already verified: a plain reshingle over good sheathing needs **no** permit. A permit is needed only for
   rot, a new layer over gapped boards, or tearing off 2+ layers.)
2. "Besides our Nebraska state contractor registration, do roofing or siding contractors need any
   **Lincoln** license or registration? Or do we just fill out your **Contractor Account Application** to
   pull permits? Is there a fee, an insurance minimum or a bond for that?"
- Bonus: "How long does a permit usually take?"

### Columbus: no call needed (answered online)

Only call if a Columbus job includes more than a re-roof or re-side, such as a deck, an addition or removing
walls. That kind of work needs a permit, and the contractor registration below.

   blocked. **Omaha stays a phone call.**
3. **Lincoln's contractor form still can't be read.** `us.openforms.com` loads now, but the form is built
   by JavaScript from a file on `usopenformsblobstorage.blob.core.windows.net`, which is still blocked. A
   headless browser failed with a certificate error, and working around that would mean turning off TLS
   checks, which we don't do. **Lincoln stays a phone call.**
4. **Lincoln clue (read directly from `lincoln.ne.gov`):** the city's "Citizen Access Information" page
   tells contractors to register with the contact type **"Licensed Professional"** (residents pick
   "Owner"). Step 4 of filing a permit is **"Select your License from the drop down."** The Building &
   Safety main page's quick links also list a **"Contractor Form"** under "Permitting and Registration".
   It's the same "Contractor Account Application" as on the Permit Applications page. What it likely means:
   even if Lincoln doesn't license roofing or siding as a trade, a contractor probably needs a **Lincoln
   contractor account** on file before pulling a permit. Question L2 asks about this directly.
5. **Lincoln office facts (read directly, verified):** open Mon-Fri 8 AM-4 PM; 555 South 10th St, Suite

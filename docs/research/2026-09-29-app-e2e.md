# HMP App: one full real working day, end to end (2026-09-29)

Builder, task `app-e2e`. FilthE knocks only after the app is proven end to end; this is the proof.

**The test:** `tests/pages/e2e_day_check.js` (in `tests/release_checks.sh`). The app in normal mode (not Practice) on a
seeded mock runtime: `tests/pages/hub_runtime_mock.js` + `tests/fixtures/app_live.json` (the engine's own morning output on
the real SPC 2026-06-13 Fremont 1.00" report replayed on 2026-09-29, real Fremont streets, made-up house numbers, no owner
names; now also the price sheet and follow-up rules the King writes, `hh.py estimate|followups --export-rules`). Every write
lands in the mock store, never the real db; the store survives reloads (sessionStorage), like the real db.
Runs 3 days at 1470x956 (MacBook Air): **EN**, **ES**, and **slow** (3 s db writes, 3 s Right Hand, mcp failing, the app
closed mid-save). Each step must leave the screen answering in under 1.5 s.

The day: 7:00 AM Now (live dot, map, hot zones, Aldaba's pick = the engine's #1) -> zones list (3) -> pick #2 -> Start
knocking -> Knock in the engine's order -> Not home / No / Interested / Booked (4 door docs; Interested = a lead with a
call-back tomorrow; Booked = inspection_set at 4 PM) -> **reload** (same tab, same next door, 4 / 20, leads still there) ->
lead: set -> inspection photo (uploaded, on the lead) -> damage notes by chat -> inspected -> quick price (roof, "Estimate
range, not final", low-high, saved to the lead) -> claim by chat (State Farm, claim #, adjuster Thu Oct 1; claim screen shows
all 3) -> paper (hard stop until the 4 must-ticks; cancel date Fri Oct 2; the cancel notice EN + ES with HMP's address; the
44-8607 notice word for word from `docs/legal/44-8607.txt`; signed_on / cancel_by saved) -> Calls today + a call sheet ->
next morning the Interested door is on the follow-up list -> chat logs doors (week stats), a lead and a claim; a "cover the
deductible" note is refused (44-8604) -> Done for today -> the review shows 4 doors / 1 interested / 1 booked and saves
`reviews/2026-09-29`.

| 1 | BLOCKER | On a slow signal, a tap still on its way to the db was lost if the app reloaded or closed (the write only went to the phone's outbox after it failed). | 2 | MAJOR | On a slow signal, a lead step's Done / ticks looked dead for seconds (the screen waited for the db echo), inviting double taps. | **Fixed**: the write shows at once (outbox overlay). Covered: `slow` day, every step checked 0.7 s after the tap. |

Covered: EN + ES. |

## Legal hard rules (checked in the test)
- 44-8604: a chat action with deductible words is refused; nothing saved. 44-8607: exact statute text on the paper step.
- 69-1601 / 69-1604(3): cancel notice EN + ES, HMP mailing address, the cancel date filled in (3 business days, Sundays and
  federal holidays skipped). Paper Done stays locked until contract + cancel said + 2 copies + deductible notice are ticked.
- Estimates always read "Estimate range, not final" / "Rango estimado, no es precio final". No owner names on door leads.

For FilthE: the test uses a replay of a real storm on made-up homes; the first real day, run it once with your own phone on
a weak signal (a parking lot at the edge of town) and watch the sync chip drain by itself.
